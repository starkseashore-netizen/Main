## 1. Who Dusekkar is

The wizard. One of the oldest beings in the setting, one of the most powerful, and — right now — one of the weakest people in the Realm.

> **[CANON]** "Dusekkar is a **retired Roblox Admin** who is intertwined with **creative** and **well-versed in magical spells**. He was a Robloxian **closely intertwined with magic and creativity itself**, who worked alongside **Builderman, Shedletsky, and Doombringer**. He was **one of the first Robloxians**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Dusekkar*](https://forsaken.wiki/Dusekkar)

> **[CANON]** "Dusekkar is **wise**, and he **knows more than he ever lets on**. **Keeping most information to himself in order to protect others.**"

That last line is his entire characterisation, and it cuts both ways. He is discreet out of care — and he is also sitting on the biggest secret in the Realm and telling no one.

### Real-world basis

**Matt Dusek**, a former **Technical Director at Roblox**. The "Dusekkar" name and wizard styling come from his long-running Roblox presence.

### Appearance

> **[CANON]** A **grey wizard outfit** — grey shirt, darker grey wizard robe over it, white robes across the body.
> **[CANON]** **Dusekkar levitates naturally, meaning he makes no footstep sounds.**

That second detail is a genuine mechanical advantage and a lovely bit of characterisation: the oldest, most depleted man on the roster is the only one who doesn't touch the ground.

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The first days

He was there at the very beginning, with **Builderman** — the two of them among the first Robloxians, building the world brick by brick.

Dusekkar's domain was different from Builderman's, though. Builderman built **infrastructure**. Dusekkar was **magic and creativity itself** — the imaginative principle of the platform given a person to live in.

> **[CANON]** He "had good relations with Shedletsky, with **Shedletsky seeing him as a brother**."

**Brother.** Not colleague, not friend. That is the relationship that defines the rest of his story.

### Watching Telamon fall

Dusekkar had a front-row seat to the worst thing that ever happened in Robloxia.

> **[CANON]** "As Telamon's power grew, so too did his ego. **Dusekkar and BrightEyes began to grow worried for him**, as his behaviour grew more reckless and self-centered by the day."

He and Shedletsky's wife — the two people closest to him — both saw it happening. Neither could stop it. Then came the unknown "utterly selfish and deplorable act," the Necrobloxicon exposure, and the split that produced **1x1x1x1**.

### The fight, and the flight

> **[CANON]** "Dusekkar's lore was vague, but he was **present during Shedletsky's fight with 1x1x1x1**, **failing to save Shedletsky** and instead **supposedly fleeing to higher grounds in SFOTH**."

This is the defining moment of Dusekkar's life, and the phrasing is merciless: **"failing to save"**, then **"fleeing."**

Consider who he was at that point. Not a bystander — **one of the first Robloxians**, an admin, a master of magic, powerful enough that losing his power later is treated as a catastrophic downgrade. He was, plausibly, the single person in the world most capable of intervening.

He went **up the mountain instead.**

And the man he abandoned was the one who **saw him as a brother.**

Two notes on fairness: the wiki says his lore here is **"vague"** and the flight is **"supposed"** — so this may be revised. And 1x1x1x1 was at **the peak of its power**, made of a legendary swordsman's concentrated hatred and Necrobloxicon corruption. Dusekkar may have been entirely right that he could not win.

But **"I would have died too"** is not a comfort you can live on, and he has had a very long time to test it.

## 3. How Dusekkar was Forsaken

The most specific abduction in the game, and one of only two the lore explains at all.

> **[CANON]** "At an unknown point afterwards, it was implied that **The Spectre had Dusekkar be Forsaken while he was asleep**."
> **[SECONDARY]** "Dusekkar was **kidnapped by the Spectre alongside his whole house** while he was sleeping."

**Asleep. In his own bed. House and all.**

No fight, no last stand, no choice. He went to sleep in his home and woke up in hell with the walls still around him.

It also tells you something about The Spectre's reach at that point: it didn't lure him or wait for a death. **It reached into the world and removed a building with a man inside it.** By this stage it wasn't hunting — it was shopping.

---

## 4. The great theft

The thing that makes Dusekkar hurt to play.

> **[CANON]** "Dusekkar is **significantly weaker than how he used to be**, with his **magic having instantly been stripped from him**."

> **[CANON]** He was once capable of "**building stuff instantly, protecting Robloxia**" but is now "**unable to cast most of his magic. Struggling to survive on his own, and needing to rely on others.**"

Set those side by side:

| Before | Now |
|:--|:--|
| Built things instantly | Casts a shield that lasts **3.5 seconds** |
| Protected all of Robloxia | Protects **one survivor at a time** |
| Among the most powerful beings alive | **Struggles to survive on his own** |
| Magic and creativity itself | **Needs other people to carry him** |

And recall the mechanism: **The Spectre "is responsible for managing and manipulating the abilities of the survivors and killers, nerfing or buffing their powers."**

So Dusekkar wasn't weakened by age or trauma. **The Spectre took it, deliberately, the instant he arrived.** It looked at the most powerful person it had collected and turned him down like a difficulty slider.

**[SECONDARY]** Dusekkar is considered **the hardest survivor in the game to play.** That is the depletion expressed as game design — the player is made to feel what it's like to be a god running on fumes.

## 5. The secret

Dusekkar is one of only two people in the Realm who knows the truth about Shedletsky and 1x1x1x1.

> **[SECONDARY]** "**Dusekkar, Shedletsky's close partner, knows everything and refuses to leak the secret.**"

He knows:

- That Telamon did something unforgivable.
- That 1x1x1x1 is Shedletsky's own hatred, walking.
- That the Killer tearing the group apart **came out of the man they all trust**.

And he says nothing — because Shedletsky is terrified of what the survivors would think, and because Dusekkar is the sort of person who "keeps most information to himself in order to protect others."

**The only other person who suspects is Jane Doe**, whose files note that Shedletsky's cheer feels forced, and who also withholds it.

So the Realm's social order rests on **two separate people independently deciding the group can't handle the truth.** That is not stable.

And there's a bleak reading of Dusekkar's silence: he isn't only protecting Shedletsky. **The story of that night includes Dusekkar running away.** He cannot tell the first half without telling the second.

### The pact

> **[SECONDARY]** "Within FORSAKEN, **Shedletsky, Builderman, and Dusekkar formed a pact to protect the Survivors at all costs.**"

Three former admins — the swordsman who made the monster, the leader whose denial made The Spectre, and the wizard who ran — swearing to protect everyone. All three doing penance. **None of them telling anyone what for.**

## 6. The kit as characterisation

**Support** — buffs and protection, the remains of a vast magical arsenal.

| Ability | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| **Spawn Protection** | Shields a chosen Survivor for up to **3.5s**, granting **Resistance IV** (80% less damage) |
| **Plasma Beam** | A beam at a Survivor (**Speed I** for 3s + **25 Overhealth**) or at the Killer (**Slowness I** for 4s) |

What the design says:

1. **He can only protect one person, for three and a half seconds.** The man who protected all of Robloxia now gets a **3.5-second window** and has to choose **who**. Every cast is a decision about who matters most right now.
2. **"Spawn Protection" is a Roblox systems term** — the invulnerability new players get on joining. He isn't casting a spell anymore; he's invoking **a platform feature**. An admin's leftover privileges, not a wizard's power.
3. **Plasma Beam is dual-purpose but weak on both ends.** Slowness I. Speed I. The lowest tiers. A man rationing what's left.
4. **He makes no footsteps.** The one gift The Spectre didn't take — because it isn't magic. It's just what he *is*.

### Jane Doe's document

Her three documents are unlocked by quests, and **Dusekkar's is titled "Once Was No Longer Is."**

Read that as a summary of him and it's devastating: *what he once was, he is no longer.* Jane Doe, a stranger, looked at him and wrote down **loss of self** as the defining fact.

Her in-character line about it survives:

> **[CANON]** "Hmm... You found my writings about Dusekkar. You're getting these findings from in there, right? That's the only way..."

## 9. The story, assembled

> He was one of the first. Before there was a Robloxia to speak of, there was him and Builderman, and they made it together — Builderman laying the structure, Dusekkar being the part that made any of it worth having. Magic and creativity itself, the wiki says, as if he weren't so much a wizard as the concept with a person attached.
>
> He could build things instantly. He protected the whole world. He was an admin, and then he retired, and he was wise, and he kept his own counsel because that was how you looked after people.
>
> And there was a young swordsman named Shedletsky who thought of him as a brother.
>
> He watched that boy become Telamon, and watched Telamon become unbearable. He and BrightEyes talked about it — both of them worried, neither able to reach him. Then Telamon did something the record refuses to name, and brushed against a book that reaches into the source code of the world, and came apart down the middle. The hatred stood up and walked away wearing a new name.
>
> And later, on the heights where the swordfighting game had been, the two halves met at the peak of their strength, and Dusekkar was there.
>
> He was, in all probability, the only person alive who might have made a difference.
>
> He went up the mountain instead.
>
> He never found out who won. Nobody did. Some time after, he went to bed in his own house, and something reached into the world and took the whole building with him asleep inside it.
>
> It stripped his magic the moment he arrived. Not worn away — taken, instantly, by the thing that tunes everyone here like numbers on a spreadsheet. The being who built things instantly can now shield one person for three and a half seconds. The being who protected Robloxia struggles to survive on his own and has to be carried by other people.
>
> Shedletsky is here too, joking and grinning and hiding what he is, and Dusekkar is the only one who knows the whole of it, and he will not say a word.
>
> He tells himself that's to protect his brother. It is. It's also that the story starts with Telamon's crime and ends with Dusekkar running.
>
> One other person has noticed something's wrong. She wrote a file on him, and she called it *Once Was No Longer Is*.

## 10. Open questions

1. **What was the deplorable act?** He knows. He's not saying.
2. **Could he have saved Shedletsky?** Unanswerable, which is why it's the right wound.
3. **Does Shedletsky know he fled?** Nothing states it. If not, the "brother" framing is even worse.
4. **Can his magic come back?** The Spectre took it. The Spectre can give things back — it balances patches.
5. **What's in Jane Doe's full document?** Only the title and a fragment of dialogue are public.
6. **What was in the house?** It came with him. Nobody says where it went.
7. **Who else is in the pact?** Three admins, sworn to protect. Undocumented in operation.
