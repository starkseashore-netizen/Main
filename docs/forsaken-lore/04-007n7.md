## 1. Who 007n7 is

Forsaken's found-family story, and the one the community latched onto hardest. A retired exploiter who tried to be a decent parent, failed in a specific and forgivable way, and lost everything for it.

> **[CANON]** "007n7 is a **reformed hacker** and **adoptive father of c00lkidd**. Once notorious for causing major destruction in Roblox's games, he's **tried to clean up his act to be a good role model for his son**, even if he wasn't perfect at it."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *007n7*](https://forsaken.wiki/007n7)

"Even if he wasn't perfect at it" is the whole character. He is not a redeemed man — he is a man **in the process of trying**, who ran out of time.

> **[SECONDARY]** "007n7 is also seen to be **very polite** as shown in his dialogue with the support Survivors, and **feels guilt over his past actions** as seen in his interactions with **Elliot** and **Builderman**."

### ⚠️ Read this first — 007n7 is the least reliable dossier here

**This is the one character whose page the Official Forsaken Wiki openly disowns.** Three problems stack up:

**1. The wiki flags the origins section as outdated.**

> **[CANON — wiki maintenance note]** 007n7 "has his own, longer and larger origins section," but **it has been marked outdated and needs a rewrite**. The wiki also notes that 007n7's and c00lkidd's origin sections **need to be synchronised with each other.**

**2. The wiki flags its own *source* as unreliable.**

> **[CANON — wiki maintenance note]** "Some information about 007n7 is **inaccurate**, and the recent **Loops controversy** has made them an **unreliable source of information** and brought **the veracity of these claims into question**. This section will need to be thoroughly researched and **possibly extensively rewritten**."

This is the sting in the tail of what I called a stabilising factor in the first version of this document. The real person behind the 007n7 and c00lkidd accounts **did** work with the dev team on this character — and that person is now the reason the wiki distrusts its own page. What looked like the strongest sourcing on the roster turns out to be the weakest.

**3. The in-house writer was replaced too.** 007n7's lore was written by a developer named **Soul**, who later acknowledged that **key parts of the characterisation were based on personal projection**. After Soul's removal, the team said publicly that 007n7 and Two Time would be **"done justice"** — Two Time has already been reassigned to a new writer.

**There is also a competing older origin.** An earlier version held that **007n7 created the c00lgui script and the c00lkidd account in late 2013** to mock online exploiters and scare children, with "Team c00lkidd" being **mainly just 007n7 and a friend** — c00lkidd as an **alt account**, not a son. The **adopted-son version is the current one** (it is what the c00lkidd page and the Swapsaken/The Kid entry both carry), but the alt-account version still circulates.

**So read everything below as a version awaiting replacement.** The load-bearing beats — a reformed exploiter, an adopted son, the c00lgui, the disappearance, the missing posters — are consistent across current pages. The finer emotional detail is exactly what a rewrite would change.

### Real-world basis

The real **007n7** created the **c00lkidd** account as an alt and developed the **c00lgui** script around **2014**, used to wreak havoc in beloved Roblox games — **Work at a Pizza Place** most famously. **Team c00lkidd** became one of the most notorious exploiter groups in Roblox history.

Forsaken's move is to take that real history and ask: *what if the alt account was a son?*

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The box on the doorstep

> **[CANON]** "c00lkidd is the adoptive child of 007n7, who **mysteriously appeared at the doorstep of 007n7's house**. Despite being **unable to handle a child**, 007n7 regardless adopted c00lkidd and took the best care of him as possible, **trying to become a better person**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *c00lkidd*](https://forsaken.wiki/C00lkidd)

**[SECONDARY]** The widely-reported version adds that the baby was found **in a box outside the front door**, abandoned **because of his skin condition** — c00lkidd's all-red, fleshy appearance.

So: a notorious criminal finds an abandoned, visibly different infant that nobody else wanted, knows he is not equipped for it, and keeps him anyway. That decision is what turns 007n7 from a hacker into a person.

### Retirement

He quit. He tried to go straight, specifically to be a better model for his son.

### The c00lgui

> **[CANON]** "c00lkidd was very mischievous though, as he managed to get his hands on **an older version of 7n7's c00lgui** while **digging through his belongings**. He would then use the c00lgui to commit **small acts of theft and arson**, not knowing the full potential of it. Despite being **caught by 7n7 from time to time** due to these shenanigans, and even **persistently hiding it**, c00lkidd still managed to get a hold of it."

This is the crux of 007n7's guilt, and it's important to be precise about it:

- He **did** hide the tools.
- He **did** keep catching his son.
- He **did** keep trying.
- **It didn't work.**

He is not guilty of neglect. He is guilty of **having been the kind of person who owned the weapon in the first place.** The sins of his old life were sitting in a box in his own house, and a curious kid found them.

> **[CANON]** "c00lkidd would later over and over again stumble upon his exploiting tools **no matter how many times he tried to hide them**."

### Team c00lkidd

> **[CANON]** "Eventually, a group of rebellious and troubled misfits would form **Team c00lkidd**, **after realizing c00lkidd's father was 007n7**. They saw him as **his successor**, and began **teaching c00lkidd more about the capabilities of the c00lgui**."

This is the part 007n7 could not have prevented, and it makes the whole thing worse. His son was recruited and radicalised **because of who his father used to be**. Strangers looked at a child and saw a legacy to inherit. His reputation reached out and took his son from him.

From there: escalating violence and destruction, with **Builder Brothers Pizza** a repeated target — to the point that both father and son were **blacklisted and permanently banned** from the establishment. See [`03-elliot.md`](03-elliot.md), whose workplace this was.

### The disappearance

> **[CANON]** "c00lkidd mysteriously **went missing**. 007n7, **broken by his son's disappearance**, would attempt to search for c00lkidd, **plastering missing posters around for help**, but **nothing would be found of his child**."

The missing posters are the detail that defines him. A man with a criminal reputation, who is presumably not welcome anywhere, walking around putting up flyers for a child that everyone else would consider a menace.

He never found him.

## 3. How 007n7 was Forsaken

> **[CANON]** "007n7 would be **Forsaken by unknown means by The Spectre** and put into its realm, being forced to play rounds with the other Survivors. Forced to play games for what feels like eternity, he has to now fight for his life alongside **the guilt of his past, the people he wronged, and his own son now being one of the many hunters trying to kill him**."

That final sentence is the wiki explicitly stating his three-part punishment:

1. **The guilt of his past** — what he did as an exploiter.
2. **The people he wronged** — who are *in the rounds with him*. Elliot. Builderman.
3. **His son hunting him.**

**[THEORY]** A widely-circulated community claim holds that 007n7 **took his own life** after losing c00lkidd, and that this is how he was forsaken. It is **not** on the official wiki, which says "unknown means." Given his lore is under revision by a team that has flagged prior projection issues, this claim should be handled with particular care. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 8.

## 4. c00lkidd's side

> **[CANON]** "He was eventually **kidnapped by a malignant force beyond comprehension**, **hoping to reconnect with his father**."

Read that again, because it reframes both characters completely.

c00lkidd wasn't taken as a punishment he understood. He was taken **while hoping to find his dad**. And in some sense he *did* — they are in the same Realm now.

But The Spectre made him a **Killer** and made his father a **Survivor**. They are in the same place, and the only way they are permitted to interact is as predator and prey.

> **[CANON]** c00lkidd is "an adopted son from a single father with **childlike curiosity**, but with the **strength of a monster**."

He is still a child. He is described as **ten years old** in the pizzeria incident. The childlike curiosity is intact — that's the horror of him. He is not evil. He is a little boy with catastrophic power and no judgement, which is exactly what he was before, just amplified.

**Design note:** c00lkidd is all red and fleshy, ribs slightly protruding, with **"team c00lkidd join today!"** plastered across his torso and two black eyes and a wide black smile **indented into his face**. The group that recruited him **wrote their advertisement on his body.**

## 5. The kit as characterisation

**Survivalist** — evasion and self-preservation. He does not fight.

| Ability | Effect | What it says |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Clone** | Creates a clone that lives 10s. On creation, 007n7 gains **Invisibility IV** and **Undetectable I** for 5s | He survives by **not being the real one** |
| **c00lgui** | Stands still for **5.5s**, then teleports to the **furthest survivor spawn** from him | Named after the tool that destroyed his family. He uses it **to run away** |
| **Inject** | Switches the movement type of his clones | The language of exploitation, repurposed defensively |

The design is pointed. **Every ability is the vocabulary of his crimes — clones, GUIs, injection — and every single one is used to escape rather than to harm.** This is a man using the exact skillset that ruined his life, and deliberately using it for nothing but survival.

The **c00lgui** ability deserves special note: it is named after his son's weapon, it requires him to **stand completely still and helpless for 5.5 seconds**, and it throws him to the **furthest point on the map**. Thematically: invoking his son costs him everything and sends him as far away as possible.

## 7. Skins as lore

c00lkidd has an unusually large skin line: **Bluudud**, **Weirdly Strict**, **PH0NK1DD**, **c00kiekidd**, **FF9CÆ**, **K00lkidd**, **Color and Electricity**, and **Mafioso**.

**Mafioso** matters beyond cosmetics: it is **one of the few semi-canonical skins** in Forsaken and is **pivotal to Chance's backstory** as **Don Sonnellino**, the mafia boss. It is also noted as one of the few characters **not** forsaken by The Spectre. See [`08-chance.md`](08-chance.md).

**Weirdly Strict** is worth a look for the found-family material specifically.

## 8. The story, assembled

> He was one of the worst people on the platform, and he was good at it. The c00lgui was his. Team c00lkidd got its name from an alt account he made. He broke things for fun and a lot of people had to clean up after him — Elliot at the pizzeria, Builderman at the top.
>
> Then somebody left a baby on his doorstep, in a box, because the baby looked wrong and nobody wanted him.
>
> He had no idea how to raise a child. He took him in anyway. He quit hacking. He tried to be someone a kid could look up to, and he wasn't perfect at it, but he was trying — which, for a man with his record, was an enormous thing.
>
> The problem was that his old life was still in the house. His son found the c00lgui while going through his things, and started small — petty theft, some arson, not really understanding what he was holding. His father caught him, took it back, hid it better. His son found it again. And again.
>
> Then strangers worked out whose kid he was, and decided he was an heir. They taught him everything his father had spent years trying to keep from him. His reputation had gone out into the world without him, and it came back and took his son.
>
> They got worse together. They got banned from the pizzeria after wrecking it so many times.
>
> And then c00lkidd was gone. No note, no body, nothing.
>
> His father put up missing posters. For a long time. A known criminal, walking around taping flyers to walls, asking people who hated him whether they'd seen his boy.
>
> He never found him.
>
> Something that eats grief was watching, and it took him too.
>
> So now he runs, in a place that never ends, using clones and injections and a program named after the thing that ruined him — not to hurt anybody, just to stay alive. Around him are the people he used to torment, and he is unfailingly polite to them, and it doesn't help.
>
> And his son is here. He was taken **hoping to find his father**, and he did.
>
> Then the thing that owns this place made one of them the prey and the other one the monster, and it's been that way ever since.

## 9. Open questions

1. **How was he Forsaken?** "Unknown means."
2. **How much of this survives the rewrite?** The team has committed to revising him.
3. **Does c00lkidd recognise him?** "Childlike curiosity" and "hoping to reconnect" suggest something is left in there. Unresolved.
4. **Who left the box?** Never addressed.
5. **Where did c00lkidd go when he vanished?** Between disappearance and abduction there's a gap.
6. **Does Elliot know?** 007n7 clearly feels it. Whether Elliot has connected the man beside him to the man who wrecked his shop is not documented.
