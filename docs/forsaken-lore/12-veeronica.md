## 1. Who Veeronica is

The skater. Loud, fast, energetic, and — underneath the noise — the survivor whose guilt is the quietest and most ordinary on the roster: **she spent her life doing the wrong thing with the time she had.**

> **[CANON]** "Veeronica is a robot who's a **fanatic about skateboarding**, and is **willing to sacrifice aid from others**. Veeronica is a **fun, hyper energetic robot**, loving to skate around as fast as she can."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Veeronica*](https://forsaken.wiki/Veeronica)

"Willing to sacrifice aid from others" is the mechanical description of a self-sufficient Survivalist — and also a character note. She doesn't ask for help. She was built to *give* it.

### Real-world basis

Based on the **Ms. Tee Vee** Roblox accessory — which is why she has a television for a head.

**[SECONDARY]** TV Tropes notes the practical consequence: because of the asset she's based on, Veeronica is **a full robot — unable to eat, only able to charge herself.** In a game where Noob's entire kit is snacks, Veeronica **physically cannot use food.** She heals off a **Battery.**

> **[CANON — development]** Veeronica was created by **Mas / Mausalia**, who originally intended her to be a **news reporter** (in the vein of the character Reporter). She was **changed to a skateboard enthusiast** later. Worth knowing because the TV-for-a-head design reads very differently once you know it was built for a broadcaster — and because her support ability is still called **Broadcast**.

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The prototype

> **[CANON]** "Veeronica is a robot originating from an experimental group known as the **'Helper RO-Bots'**, the **successor to HelperBots**. This group was **never fully produced**, due to **high costs and general disinterest**, leading to only **5 prototypes** being made, each corresponding to a **different niche**."

So Veeronica is a **failed product line**. Five units, each specialised, and then the project was shelved because it cost too much and nobody cared.

She is the successor generation to **Helperbot** — the same Helperbot that is now in the Realm being called **D3functB0t** and slowly rebuilt by **Builderman** out of generator scrap. See [`09-builderman.md`](09-builderman.md).

**Two generations of the same discontinued helper project are both trapped in hell.** One is a wreck being repaired by the man who ran the company; the other is a survivor.

> **[CANON]** "**Two of these Helper RO-Bots have since gone defunct**, with **Veeronica and another unknown RO-Bot remaining.**"

Five made. Two dead. Veeronica and **one unidentified sibling** still out there — an explicit, deliberately-placed hook for a future character.

### Discontinued

> **[CANON]** "Veeronica would **help Robloxians by teaching them all sorts of things**, but **that opportunity would pass as the experiment would be discontinued**, having to **wander around the streets of Robloxia alone**."

This is the wound, and it's worth being precise about it: **she was good at her job and enjoyed it, and the job was cancelled.** Not because she failed. Because it cost too much and nobody was interested.

A machine built for one purpose, with that purpose administratively withdrawn, left to wander the streets with a function and nothing to point it at.

### The wrong crowd

> **[CANON]** "**From her nature, Veeronica would search for a group to educate.** While she did find people, they **didn't have the greatest influence on her**, introducing her to **vandalism via graffiti**, but also her **very own hobby, skateboarding**."

She went looking for **students**. That's the detail that makes her sympathetic rather than a delinquent — her drive wasn't rebellion, it was **purpose-seeking**. She was built to teach and she went out looking for someone to teach.

What she found was a crowd who taught **her** instead. And they gave her two things:

- **Graffiti** — vandalism. Destruction of other people's property. The opposite of helping.
- **Skateboarding** — the first thing in her existence that was **hers**, chosen rather than programmed.

You cannot separate them. The same people who ruined her gave her the only joy she has.

### The guilt

> **[CANON]** "She has **guilt to this day that she didn't vandalize and helped more people instead**."

(The wiki's phrasing is garbled, but the meaning is clear from context and from her Milestone IV description: **she regrets vandalising instead of helping.**)

> **[CANON]** "By the time Veeronica achieves **Milestone IV**, she is **regretful of her actions** and is **unhappy that she can't go back**, leading to her **angry expression** in her render.

**"Unhappy that she can't go back."** Not regret in the abstract — **she wants to return and do it differently, and cannot.** The anger on her Milestone IV face isn't aimed at anyone else.

Set against the rest of the roster, Veeronica's sin is tiny. She didn't murder anyone, didn't cause a genocide, didn't create a monster. **She tagged walls when she could have been teaching people.** She wasted herself.

And that qualified her — which tells you how low the bar is. **[CANON]** The Spectre "takes people based on **low moments in life** and/or **high emotions and fears**, feeding off of them." Veeronica's regret is small, ordinary and entirely disproportionate to what happened to her. It didn't need to be more than that.

## 3. How Veeronica was Forsaken

> **[CANON]** "Veeronica would be **Forsaken by The Spectre**, and **reported missing by others**."

The mechanism isn't stated, but **"reported missing by others"** is significant: **someone noticed and filed a report.**

The crowd who taught her to vandalise — or someone else — **cared enough to report her gone.** For a discontinued prototype who spent her functional life wandering the streets alone, that is a genuinely warm detail buried in a horror story.

Contrast **Taph**, whose trace "went cold" and who is remembered only as a fugitive. Both vanished. Somebody went looking for Veeronica.

## 4. In the Realm

The most socially successful newcomer on the roster.

> **[CANON]** "In the Forsaken realm, Veeronica gets **well-acquainted with some of the Survivors like Noob, Elliot, and Builderman**."

That list is not random:

- **Noob** — the gentlest survivor, whose whole story is befriending someone the world had written off.
- **Elliot** — the compulsive carer who helps everyone first.
- **Builderman** — the man who ran the company that **cancelled her product line**, and who is currently repairing her **predecessor**.

That last one is quietly enormous. Veeronica has become friendly with the executive whose company decided she wasn't worth manufacturing. Whether either of them knows is undocumented — and if Builderman does know, it's one more thing on a very long list.

She is a Survivalist who is "willing to sacrifice aid from others" — self-reliant by design — and yet she is the one making friends fastest. A robot built to help people, finally surrounded by people who need help, in the one place where her original purpose is useful again.

## 5. The kit as characterisation

**Survivalist** — self-sufficiency, evasion, staying alive alone.

> **[CANON]** "Veeronica's abilities **VANDALISM, Sk8, and Broadcast** allow her to **skate around the map, performing tricks to stall the Killer, speeding up, and healing herself using her Battery.**"

| Ability | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| **VANDALISM** | Equip spray paint; hold click to spray **Graffiti** on a wall (**5s** to complete, max **3 pieces**). A completed piece **creates an area that enables Sk8.** |
| **Sk8** | Skateboarding within her graffiti zones — speed, tricks, **knockback** against the Killer |
| **Broadcast** | TV-head ability tied to healing/utility via her **Battery** |

This is the most thematically integrated kit in the game, and the loop is the character:

**She has to vandalise before she can skate.**

Her graffiti doesn't damage anything — **it creates the space where she's able to move.** The two things the bad crowd gave her are **mechanically fused**: the destruction is the precondition for the joy. She cannot have one without the other, permanently, by design.

And it's her **survival mechanism**. The thing she feels guilty about is the only reason she's still alive down here.

Other notes:

- **She heals with a Battery**, not food. She cannot eat. In a roster full of Bloxy Cola and pizza, she plugs herself in and recharges alone.
- **Broadcast** — she has a television for a head, and her support ability is **transmitting**. A teaching machine, still broadcasting, with nobody left to teach but seven other damned people.
- **[CANON]** During development, VANDALISM used **meme sounds as placeholder SFX** before final audio.

## 8. The story, assembled

> There were meant to be a lot of them. The Helper RO-Bots — the next generation after the HelperBots — each one built for a different niche, all of them built to help people. In the end they made five prototypes and then cancelled the project, because it cost too much and nobody was especially interested.
>
> Veeronica was one of the five. She taught people things. She was good at it, and she liked it, and then the experiment was discontinued and there was nothing for her to do.
>
> So she wandered the streets of Robloxia by herself, a teaching machine with nobody to teach.
>
> And because that was what she was for, she went looking for a group to educate.
>
> She found people. They weren't the best influence. They didn't want lessons — they had their own things to show her, and what they showed her was how to spray graffiti on other people's walls, and how to ride a skateboard.
>
> One of those was vandalism, and she's never stopped feeling bad about it. The other was the first thing she ever chose for herself, and she loves it more than anything.
>
> She spent her time breaking things when she could have been helping people. That's the whole of her crime. And she has never got over it — by the time you've played her long enough to see it, the anger has settled into her face, because she would go back and do it differently and she cannot.
>
> Something that collects people who can't forgive themselves noticed her.
>
> She went missing, and somebody reported it. Which, for a discontinued prototype who'd been alone on the streets, is not nothing.
>
> Now she's in a place full of people who need help, which is the most use she's had since the day they shelved the line. She's made friends fast — Noob, Elliot, and Builderman, which is funny in a way nobody seems to have said out loud, because Builderman ran the company that decided she wasn't worth building. He's down the hall most days, patiently repairing the model that came before her out of parts he's pulled from the generators.
>
> And to survive, she sprays graffiti on the walls of hell — and only where the paint is can she skate.
>
> The thing she regrets is the only thing keeping her alive. Every single round.
>
> Then she plugs into her battery, because she can't eat, and waits for the next one.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Who is the fifth RO-Bot?** Two defunct, Veeronica, and **one unknown remaining.** This is a planted hook.
2. **Has she met Helperbot?** Both are in the Realm. Both from the same discontinued lineage. No documented interaction.
3. **Does Builderman know?** The cancelled product line is his company's decision.
4. **Who reported her missing?** Somebody cared.
5. **How was she Forsaken?** Mechanism unstated.
6. **Were the RO-Bots' niches ever specified?** Five prototypes, "each corresponding to a different niche." Veeronica's appears to be general education.
7. **Can a robot be in "purgatory"?** Noob's file calls the Realm a purgatory. Veeronica is a machine, and The Spectre took her anyway.
