## 1. Who Guest 1337 is

The soldier. The only survivor on the roster who is, unambiguously, a **war hero** — and the only one whose documented abduction begins with a confirmed death.

Guest 1337 is Forsaken's argument that doing everything right does not save you. He lost his family, lost his species, fought a war for them, won it, and paid with his life. And then the universe took the corpse and put it back to work.

### Real-world basis

**Guests** were added to Roblox on **26 September 2008** — accountless players who could join games without signing up. They were **removed in October 2017** (PC first, mobile later) because moderating players with no account was effectively impossible, and the feature was heavily abused by exploiters.

**ObliviousHD's *The Last Guest*** turned that patch note into an elegy: a machinima series about one of the last remaining Guests on Roblox, fighting a **Bacon Soldier** terrorist organisation that had systematically massacred the guest population.

> **[CANON]** "Guest 1337's lore is consistent with the **first part** of ObliviousHD's *The Last Guest* series."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Guest 1337*](https://forsaken.wiki/Guest_1337)

Note **"the first part."** Forsaken adopts the first film and then **diverges** — because in Forsaken, what happens after the grenade is that The Spectre takes him.

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The child

> **[CANON]** "Guest 1337 was the **son of two unnamed guests** who faced **discrimination for their race**. Despite the adversity he faced, he was taught from an early age to **always stay true to himself** and to **'be strong, always be strong.'**"

**"Be strong, always be strong"** is the load-bearing line of the character. It is the last thing his parents taught him, and it is the philosophy that gets him killed.

> **[CANON]** "His parents were **murdered in cold blood by the Bacon General** during a **playground visit**."

At a playground. In front of him. By the man he would eventually kill.

> **[CANON]** "He was sent to an **orphanage** where he befriended **Daisy** and **Matt**... with the three becoming **inseparable**."

Daisy and Matt are the family he built after the one he lost. **Matt** has a dedicated skin line in Forsaken (**Matt**, **Modern Matt**), which tells you how central he is.

### The soldier

> **[CANON]** "Guest 1337 is a **soldier of the Roblox Army** who enlisted to fight against the **Bacon Empire terrorists** after the **genocide of his people**."

Two things worth connecting here, because the wiki links them:

> **[CANON]** "Following the **disappearance of the admins caused by The Spectre**, the Bacon Empire **declared war against Guests** and anyone who supported them."

**The Spectre caused this war.** Not directly — it didn't order the genocide. But by removing the admins, it removed the only thing holding the Bacon Empire back. Guest 1337's parents, his people, his war and his death all sit **downstream of the corruption in Roblox HQ's servers**.

Which means the entity that eventually claimed him is the same entity that created the circumstances that killed everyone he loved. It manufactured his grief and then collected it.

> **[CANON]** "The conflict with the Bacon Empire escalated into an **all-out war**, with the Bacon General's forces aiming to take control of **all of Roblox** through military force."

## 3. How Guest 1337 was Forsaken

The clearest death in the survivor roster.

> **[CANON]** "Guest 1337 **detonated a hand grenade at point blank** and **ignited an oil tank**, causing an explosion that **killed the Bacon General** during the Bacon War. **To the rest of the world, however, Guest 1337 was dead.** ... Guest 1337 was **taken by The Spectre**, making him a competitor in its Forsaken realm."

Break it down:

1. **He killed the man who murdered his parents.** Personally. Face to face.
2. **He did it as a suicide attack.** Point-blank grenade, oil tank, no escape planned.
3. **He won the war.** The Bacon General is dead; the invasion is over.
4. **He died.** Publicly, confirmed, mourned.
5. **The Spectre took him anyway.**

This is why Guest 1337 is the strongest single piece of evidence for the "survivors are already dead" reading. His death is not implied or theorised — it is stated, and TV Tropes lists him alongside Dusekkar as **one of only two survivors whose abduction is explained at all.**

The cruelty is specific. He chose to die, and chose *how*, and chose *what for* — the single most meaningful act available to him. **The Spectre voided it.** The one thing he earned was an ending, and it was taken.

And back home, **he is still a hero and still dead.** Daisy and Matt buried him. Nobody is looking for him. Unlike 007n7's missing posters or Jane Doe's search, **there is no rescue narrative for Guest 1337**, because everyone who loved him already knows what happened and has grieved it.

## 4. In the Realm

A soldier in a war with no front line, no objective, and no victory condition.

He is on the **Lobby corkboard** alongside Noob, Shedletsky, Builderman, 007n7, Taph, Elliot and Dusekkar. As a **Sentinel** he fights the Killer directly to keep others alive — which is exactly what he did in life, for exactly as long as it worked.

**[SECONDARY]** He is considered the **hardest Sentinel** in the game to use (the hardest survivor overall being Dusekkar). Fitting: his kit demands precise timing and real commitment, and punishes hesitation.

---

## 5. The kit as characterisation

**Sentinel** — combat, stunning, body-blocking.

| Ability | Effect | What it says |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Block** | **Resistance V (invincibility)** for 1s; movement reduced 80% for 2s | He plants himself and takes the hit. The invincibility is real but tiny, and he's nearly immobile afterward — *"be strong"*, with the cost attached |
| **Charge** | Dashes forward in a shoulder-bash for 1.5s; on contact, knocks the enemy back and **locks their abilities for 1.25s** | He closes distance with a monster on purpose |
| **Punch** | Reward condition: **successfully punching the Killer** | He has no weapon. He hits it with his hands. |

Guest 1337 is the only survivor whose entire kit is **his own body**. No gadgets, no tools, no consumables — block, charge, punch. A soldier who lost his war, his weapons, and his death, and still walks at the thing.

**Block** is the most characterful ability in the game. One second of genuine invincibility, then two seconds of being nearly unable to move. You cannot use it to escape. You can only use it to **be hit instead of someone else.**

Note what's missing: **no antagonist in the Realm is personally his.** Noob has Guest 666. Shedletsky has 1x1x1x1. 007n7 has c00lkidd. Two Time has Azure. Guest 1337's nemesis is **already dead, by his own hand**. He finished his story before he got here. He is the only survivor with nothing left to resolve — which may be exactly why he is so hard to play.

## 8. The story, assembled

> He was born to two people that the world had decided did not count, and they taught him anyway: stay true to yourself, and be strong, always be strong.
>
> Then one afternoon at a playground, the Bacon General killed them both in front of him.
>
> He went to an orphanage. He met Matt and Daisy, and the three of them were inseparable, and for a while that was enough. But outside the orphanage his people were being hunted to extinction — because the admins had vanished, one by one, taken by something in the servers nobody had fixed, and with them gone the Bacon Empire declared open season on Guests.
>
> So he enlisted. Of course he did. He'd been told to be strong.
>
> The war escalated until the Bacon Empire was trying to take all of Roblox by force, and at the end of it Guest 1337 found himself in front of the man who had murdered his parents, and he did the arithmetic.
>
> He pulled the pin at point-blank range and set off an oil tank, and the Bacon General died, and so did he.
>
> That was supposed to be the end. He avenged his parents, he saved what was left of his people, and he paid for it with everything he had. Back home he is a hero. Back home he is buried. Matt and Daisy grieved him and moved on, because there was nothing else to do.
>
> But something had been watching the whole war — something that had started it by accident, and had been eating the grief the entire time — and it did not accept his ending.
>
> So it took him.
>
> Now he is in a place with no front line and no victory condition, and the only tools he has left are his own two hands. He blocks, and he charges, and he punches. One second of invincibility at a time, then two seconds where he can barely move.
>
> He cannot win. He knows he cannot win.
>
> He steps in front of people anyway. Be strong. Always be strong.

## 9. Open questions

1. **What happened to Matt and Daisy?** Alive in the world he left, presumably. Neither is in the Realm.
2. **Does he know the war is over and won?** If he died on impact, he may not know the General died too.
3. **How much of *The Last Guest* is canon?** "The first part" is explicit. Later entries in the series are not.
4. **Does he know The Spectre caused the war?** Nobody appears to have told him the admins' disappearance is why his people were hunted.
5. **Why is he the hardest Sentinel?** A design choice that reads as thematic: he requires you to commit to protecting people at your own expense.
