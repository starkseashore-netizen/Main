## 1. Who Shedletsky is

The swordsman. On the surface: confident, lighthearted, heroic — the veteran fighter who puts himself between the Killer and everyone else. He is a free starter character, so most players learn the game while playing him, which makes the reveal underneath land harder.

Because the confidence is a performance, and at least one person in the Realm has already noticed.

> **[CANON]** Jane Doe claims in her files that his smiles and lighthearted demeanour **feel forced**.

Shedletsky is the roster's best-constructed tragedy: a man being hunted by his own worst quality, given physical form, who cannot tell anyone why — because the moment he does, he loses the only thing he has left.

### Real-world basis

**John Shedletsky** was Roblox's Creative Director and one of the platform's foundational figures. His in-game alias **Telamon** was legendary, and he created **Sword Fights on the Heights (SFOTH)**, one of the defining early Roblox experiences. He also co-wrote **the Chronicles of Roblox** with **MrDoombringer** — the lore series that introduced the **Necrobloxicon**.

That last fact is the game's sharpest piece of metafiction: **the artifact Shedletsky helped invent in real life is the artifact that destroys him in Forsaken.**

**[SECONDARY]** The real John Shedletsky has been **positive about the game and has reposted Forsaken content on X.**

## 2. The rise

> **[CANON]** "Shedletsky was recruited by the admins early on during the construction of Robloxia, serving as their **creative director**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Shedletsky*](https://forsaken.wiki/Shedletsky)

He built **Sword Fights on the Heights**, a fighting game where players duelled with the standard **Linked Sword** or with better swords scattered around the map. And he played in it himself, under the name **Telamon**, where he built a reputation for **superior fighting skill**.

Everything went right at once:

> **[CANON]** "His game was flourishing, his Telamon persona was popular with the community, he was becoming good friends with **Dusekkar** and **Builderman**, and he even fell in love with a bright young woman named **BrightEyes**, whom he married soon afterwards."

**BrightEyes** is drawn from a real person: **Christina Shedletsky**, a former Roblox administrator who joined 11 June 2008, ran Customer Service, later became Director of Advertising until departing in 2018, and has been married to John Shedletsky since 2014.

---

## 3. The fall

The wiki does not soften this.

> **[CANON]** "However, he was still **young, foolish, and arrogant**: he began to **abandon his duties as an admin** in pursuit of honing his swordsmanship and gaining more power."

This is the critical distinction in Shedletsky's character. **Telamon is not a separate person.** Telamon is Shedletsky with the brakes off — the part of him that wanted to be the best swordsman in Robloxia more than he wanted to do his job.

> **[CANON]** "As Telamon's power grew, so too did his ego. **Dusekkar and BrightEyes began to grow worried for him**, as his behaviour grew more reckless and self-centered by the day."

His two closest people — his best friend and his wife — both saw it happening and could not stop it.

### The act

Then the wiki draws a curtain, and the curtain is the single biggest hole in Forsaken's lore.

> **[CANON]** "This reached a breaking point in an **unknown incident** that led Telamon to perform an **utterly selfish and deplorable act**."

That is all we get. No details. Not a hint at the nature of it. The phrasing — "utterly selfish and deplorable" — is unusually harsh for a wiki describing a playable hero, and it is doing a lot of deliberate work.

### The split

> **[CANON]** "This act, combined with Telamon's **incidental exposure to the Necrobloxicon artifact**, caused him to **physically separate from his hatred** and create a monster comprised of the hatred and hubris of his now-shattered identity of Telamon: **1x1x1x1**."

Two causes, both required:

1. **The deplorable act** — the moral rupture.
2. **The Necrobloxicon** — the cursed book with access to the **Lua code the Roblox world runs on**, which turned a psychological break into a *physical* one.

1x1x1x1 is not a demon that possessed him and not an enemy he made. **It is the hatred and hubris of Telamon, walking around on its own**, carved out of a self that had already shattered.

The identity "Telamon" does not survive the process. What is left is **Shedletsky** — the man minus the arrogance, minus the ambition, and minus the part of him that did the unforgivable thing. Which sounds like redemption until you realise the removed part is still out there, and still angry, and still knows exactly whose fault it is.

## 4. BrightEyes

The other curtain.

The wiki confirms he married her and that she grew worried about him. It does not, in what is publicly documented, confirm what became of her.

The strongest available signal is in a skin description:

> **[CANON — skin lore, non-canonical status]** The **Hacklord (1x1x1x1)** skin is based on a transformed version of the **Heartbroken Shedletsky**, who kills **over the death/banning of his wife BrightEyes**.

Handle this with care. Skins are explicitly "what-if" alternate realities. Hacklord is therefore evidence that **losing BrightEyes is a thing that happens to Shedletsky in at least one branch** — but it is not proof of the main timeline.

Still: for a "what-if" to be legible as a what-if, the reader must recognise the loss it is riffing on. And there is a **BrightEyes (Shedletsky)** skin, plus a dedicated `Shedletsky/BrightEyes` page — so she is a persistent, load-bearing presence in his material.

**[THEORY]** The most common fandom reading is that BrightEyes' death or banning **is the unknown deplorable act** — either Telamon caused it, or his negligence allowed it. Evidence is circumstantial. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 5.

## 5. The fight, and the taking

> **[CANON]** "At some unknown point in the future, Shedletsky would be forced to face off against 1x1x1x1 **at the peak of their power**. The outcome of this event is **currently unknown**; it is evident that regardless of the fight's conclusion, however, **both of them would eventually be taken by The Spectre** to engage in an **endless cycle of gladiatorial violence**."

Three things worth pulling out:

1. **Who won is not recorded.** Deliberately.
2. **It did not matter.** Win or lose, The Spectre took them both.
3. **"Endless cycle of gladiatorial violence"** is the wiki describing the actual game loop — in-universe, Shedletsky and 1x1x1x1 are made to fight *forever*, and the rounds you play are those fights.

**Dusekkar was there.**

> **[CANON]** "Dusekkar's lore was vague, but he was present during Shedletsky's fight with 1x1x1x1, **failing to save Shedletsky** and instead supposedly **fleeing to higher grounds in SFOTH**."

The best friend watched, couldn't help, and ran. He is now a Support class in the same rounds, and he is keeping Shedletsky's secret. See [`10-dusekkar.md`](10-dusekkar.md).

## 6. The secret, and who knows

The live tension of Shedletsky's character in the present day.

> **[CANON]** "Shedletsky is **terrified** of 1x1x1x1 and subsequently what the survivors would think of him if they figured out the two were linked, so he's decided to keep quiet on the matter, as he **wants the survivors to trust him, not hate him**."

He is not hiding it out of pride. He is hiding it because he is frightened of being alone in here.

**Who knows what:**

| Character | Knowledge |
|:--|:--|
| **Dusekkar** | **Knows everything.** Refuses to leak it. Shedletsky's close partner. |
| **Jane Doe** | **Suspects.** Her files say his cheer feels forced. She has worked out something is wrong and **also withholds it** from the others. |
| **Noli** (Killer) | **[SECONDARY]** Believed to be the only Killer aware — and suspected to know only part of the truth. |
| **The other Survivors** | Nothing. They trust him. |
| **1x1x1x1** | Increasingly — see below. |

Two people are covering for him, independently, for different reasons. Dusekkar out of loyalty; Jane Doe out of judgement about what the group can bear. That is not a stable arrangement, and it is clearly built to collapse.

### 1x1x1x1's side

> **[SECONDARY]** "As 1x1x1x1 continued to grow stronger, **fragments of memory began to surface** — not from their own, but as an **intensifying hatred directed solely at Shedletsky**. Every time 1x1x1x1 confronts Shedletsky, their **'hearts' tug and beat in unison**, reflecting their deep connection despite 1x1x1x1's overwhelming hatred for him."

The monster is **remembering**. Not memories of its own life — it doesn't have one — but Telamon's memories, arriving as pure hatred with Shedletsky's name attached. And the hearts-in-unison detail is the game quietly confirming the link at a physical level: whatever was cut apart is still, mechanically, **one thing**.

## 7. The kit as characterisation

**Sentinel** — combat, stunning, body-blocking. He fights the Killer so others don't have to.

| Ability | Effect | What it says |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Slash** | Stuns the Killer for several seconds, stalling for time | The swordsmanship that ruined his life is now the only useful thing he has |

He wields **Illumina** — the legendary sword from his own game.

The irony is complete: Shedletsky abandoned his responsibilities to become the greatest swordsman in Robloxia. That pursuit split him in half and got him damned. And now the *only* way he can protect anyone is with the exact skill that cost him everything. He has to keep being Telamon, in the narrow useful sense, forever — while insisting he isn't.

## 9. The story, assembled

> He was there at the beginning. Recruited early, made creative director, trusted with helping build the world. He made a game about sword fighting on a mountain, and then he went and played it himself under another name, and he was *extraordinary* at it.
>
> Telamon won. Telamon kept winning. And the better he got, the less he cared about anything else — the admin work, the responsibilities, the people. His best friend noticed. His wife noticed. Neither could reach him.
>
> Then he did something. The record refuses to say what, only that it was utterly selfish and deplorable. And around the same time he brushed against an ancient cursed book with its hands in the world's source code.
>
> The two things together broke him in half. Not metaphorically — the hatred and the arrogance came *out* of him and stood up as a separate creature. Telamon ceased to exist. What was left was Shedletsky, and what walked away was 1x1x1x1.
>
> They fought eventually, at the height of both their powers. Dusekkar was there, and couldn't save him, and ran. No record survives of who won, because it never mattered: something older than both of them was watching, and it wanted them both.
>
> Now they fight forever. And Shedletsky stands in front of the others with his legendary sword and his easy grin, keeping them alive, keeping them laughing — and never, ever telling them that the thing tearing them apart is the part of himself he couldn't keep.
>
> Two people have worked it out. Both are letting him lie.

## 10. Open questions

1. **What was the deplorable act?** The central unanswered question of Forsaken's lore.
2. **What happened to BrightEyes?** Death, banning, or something else — and was Shedletsky responsible?
3. **Who won the fight?** Explicitly withheld, which usually means it's being saved.
4. **Can 1x1x1x1 be reintegrated?** If it is literally his hatred, is the endgame reabsorption or destruction — and does he survive either?
5. **When does the secret break?** Two people knowingly covering is a structure designed to fail.
6. **Is Noli's partial knowledge leverage?** A Killer who knows something a Survivor is hiding is a plot device with a safety pin in it.
