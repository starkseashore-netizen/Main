# Sources, and How Much to Trust Them

> Forsaken has a very large fandom and a very active SEO-spam problem. This page is a guide to which sources are worth citing.

---

## Tier 1 — Primary / authoritative

### The Official Forsaken Wiki — `forsaken.wiki`

**The primary source for everything in this compendium.**

The game's official wiki. Detailed, actively maintained, and the source of nearly every **[CANON]** tag in these documents.

**Caveats:**
- It changes frequently as lore is written and rewritten.
- **Editorial inconsistency is visible** — most obviously in pronoun usage, where the same character is "he," "she," and "they" in consecutive sentences. Treat the *facts* as reliable and the *prose* as multi-author.
- Some phrasing is garbled (Veeronica's guilt sentence is a good example) and requires reading in context.

### Developer statements

**The highest authority in Forsaken**, because the wiki itself says so:

> "Currently the main source for the lore behind characters are through **Developers giving information about it**."

Channels: the **Forsaken Discord** (developer **Vess** has confirmed character genders/pronouns there — the wiki hosts `File:Taph_gender_citation.png` as a receipt) and **X/Twitter**.

**Problem:** Discord statements are poorly archived. Much of what the community "knows" traces back to a dev message in a channel that is hard to re-verify. Where a claim traces only to Discord, this compendium tags it **[DEV]** and says so.

### The game itself

Jane Doe's document quests, Lobby NPC dialogue, the corkboard, achievement names ("Mold Girl"), Last Man Standing themes, skin descriptions. Primary, but thin — most lore is not yet in-game.

### The ARG

Source for **Tyler**, **David**, and the encoded **Guest 666** letters. Canon, but scattered and community-decoded.

## Tier 2 — Reliable secondary

### TV Tropes — *Forsaken (Roblox)*

Surprisingly **the best secondary source available**, and in some areas better organised than the official wiki. Particularly strong on development history, canon status of skins, and who wrote what.

|:--|:--|

| [WMG](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/WMG/ForsakenRoblox) | Fan theories, clearly labelled as such |
| [Headscratchers](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Headscratchers/ForsakenRoblox) | Plot holes and inconsistencies |

**Caveat:** TV Tropes is user-edited and mixes documentation with opinion. It is excellent on *development facts* and weaker on *in-fiction facts*, where the official wiki should win any conflict.

### FORSAKEN Wiki (Fandom) — `forsaken2024.fandom.com`

The **older, larger, messier** community wiki, predating `forsaken.wiki`.

**Good for:** cross-checking, real-world background on the Roblox history being adapted, and occasionally richer detail than the official wiki (the c00lkidd rocket-launcher incident, Elliot's motive for working, the Shedletsky "who knows the secret" breakdown).

**Bad for:** telling articles apart from **fan content**. The wiki hosts an enormous volume of user blogs, AU pages, "remakes," and forum posts that look like articles in search results. Anything under `/f/p/`, `/f/t/` or `User_blog:` is **a forum post or blog, not documentation**.

Examples of pages that are *not* lore despite appearing in searches: *"Updated 007n7 lore in my au lol"*, *"SWAP AU: 007N7"*, *"FORSAKEN REMAKE"*, *"007n7 but editable"*, *"taph but you can fucking ruin it"*.

### Other Fandom wikis

[Heroes Wiki](https://hero.fandom.com/wiki/Survivors_(Forsaken)) · [Villains Wiki](https://villains.fandom.com/wiki/The_Spectre_(Forsaken)) · [Roblox Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Dev_Team)

**Use sparingly.** These are general-purpose character-catalogue wikis. They summarise Forsaken's lore accurately most of the time but sometimes state the **strongest possible version** of a claim without hedging — the "Collapse of Robloxia / extinction of the Robloxian race" framing comes from Villains Wiki and does not appear in that form on `forsaken.wiki`.

**Avoid entirely for lore:** power-scaling wikis (*Alt Battles*, *All Fiction Battles*, *Roblox Verse Scaling*). They exist to rank characters in hypothetical fights and will assert capabilities that have no basis in the game.

### NamuWiki — `en.namu.wiki`

Has detailed Forsaken pages, including a controversies/incidents page. Machine-translated from Korean; quality varies. Useful as a cross-check, not a citation.

**Rule of thumb:** a lore video is only as good as its sourcing. Videos that cite the wiki and dev statements are useful. Videos that narrate confidently without sourcing are usually recycling other videos.

### Forums and discussion

- **FORSAKEN Wiki (Fandom) discussion boards** — genuine community theorising, e.g. ["My theory on who the Spectre is"](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000144395), ["Hyper analyzing Forsaken Lore 1"](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000219042), ["whats the lore of this game"](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000143327), ["Possible explanations about how characters were sent into forsaken"](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000205740). **Discussion, not documentation.**
- **r/ForsakenRoblox** and related subreddits — active, but Reddit blocks automated crawlers, so it could not be surveyed for this compendium. **Noted as a gap.**
- **The official Forsaken Discord** — where developers actually answer lore questions. The most valuable community venue and the least citable.
- **X/Twitter** — dev posts, plus fan-run accounts like [@ForsakenWikiFD](https://x.com/ForsakenWikiFD) that surface datamines and teasers. **Datamined/unreleased content is not lore.**

### TikTok

Enormous volume of Forsaken lore content. Overwhelmingly **headcanon, AU material, and repetition of other people's videos**, usually with no sourcing at all. Useful for gauging what the fandom believes; **not usable as a source**.

## Tier 4 — Do not use

### AI-generated and SEO content farms

Searching "Forsaken characters" surfaces a layer of auto-generated games-content sites. **These are actively misleading.**

A real example encountered during this research — one such site listed the Forsaken survivor roster as:

> *"Chance, Guest, Noob, John Doe, Jane Doe, Veeronica, Demo, Host, Invicible, Mike, Posty, Conflictor, and Swiftshift"*

**Demo, Host, Invicible, Mike, Posty, Conflictor and Swiftshift are not Forsaken characters.** They appear to be fabricated or imported from an unrelated game. The same article also listed **John Doe** — a Killer — as a Survivor.

Sites in this category encountered during research included `forsakenhub.com`, `isekaizero.ai`, `rosenberryrooms.com`, and `driffle.com`. Treat any games-content site with a "(2026)" in the title and no cited sources as unreliable by default.

**How to spot them:** confident tone, no sources, character names you've never seen on the official wiki, "ranked" or "tier list" framing, and generated-looking prose.

### Power-scaling wikis

*Alt Battles*, *All Fiction Battles*, *Roblox Verse Scaling*. They document hypothetical combat capability, not narrative. They will confidently assert things the game has never said.

### AU / fan-content wikis

`forsaken-but-bad.fandom.com`, `forsaken2025.fandom.com`, and similar. **These are fan-project wikis**, not documentation of the real game. They appear in search results next to real sources and are easy to mistake for them.

## The verification checklist

Before treating any Forsaken lore claim as fact:

1. **Is it on `forsaken.wiki`?** If yes, it's canon (as of today).
2. **If not — is it attributed to a named developer?** If yes, likely canon, possibly ahead of the wiki.
3. **Is it from a skin or milestone?** Then it's probably **"what-if"** and not canon. Exceptions: Two Time's *Nightshade*, c00lkidd's *Mafioso*.
4. **Does it concern 007n7 or Two Time?** Their lore has been **rewritten or flagged for rewriting**. Verify the current version.
5. **Does the source list any sources?** If not, assume it's downstream of a YouTube video that was downstream of the wiki.
6. **Does it name a character you can't find on the official wiki?** It's probably fabricated.

## Research note on this compendium

This dossier was assembled in an environment where **direct web page fetching was blocked by network policy** — `forsaken.wiki`, Fandom, TV Tropes and others all returned `403` at the egress proxy. Research was therefore conducted through **search-engine retrieval**, which reaches those domains server-side and returns extracted content.

**What this means for you:**

- Quotes are reproduced faithfully from retrieved content and are attributed to the specific page they came from.
- **Full page text was not directly read**, so some sections of some pages may be unrepresented — the gaps flagged as "check this live" throughout are genuine.
- The official wiki changes frequently. **Before citing anything here in an argument, open the live page.**

Pages specifically identified as existing but not fully retrieved, worth reading directly: [`Taph/Intelligence`](https://forsaken.wiki/Taph/Intelligence), [`Elliot/Friend`](https://forsaken.wiki/Elliot/Friend), the full text of Jane Doe's three documents, and the [`Lore`](https://forsaken.wiki/Lore) page in its entirety.
