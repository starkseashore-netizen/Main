# The Forsaken Survivor Lore Compendium

> A research dossier on the lore of the Roblox horror game **Forsaken** (Forsaken Dev Team, released 25 December 2024), focused on the twelve playable **Survivors**.

---

## What this is

Twelve long-form character dossiers, plus a world-lore primer and a theory file. Every claim is sourced, and every claim is graded so you can tell **what the game actually says** apart from **what the fandom has decided it says**.

Forsaken has one of the largest headcanon/fanwork ecosystems on Roblox. A huge amount of what circulates as "Forsaken lore" on TikTok, in AU blogs, and in fanfic is not lore at all. These documents deliberately exclude that material from the lore sections and quarantine the speculative material into a clearly-labelled theory file.

## The evidence grading used throughout

| Tag | Meaning |
|:--|:--|
| **[CANON]** | Stated on the Official Forsaken Wiki (`forsaken.wiki`) or shown in-game, outside an "outdated" banner. |
| **[DEV]** | Stated directly by a Forsaken developer (Discord, X, interviews). Canon, but not always written up on the wiki yet. |
| **[SECONDARY]** | Reported by a non-official but reputable documentation wiki (FORSAKEN Fandom wiki, TV Tropes). Usually correct; occasionally out of date. |
| **[THEORY]** | Community interpretation. Not canon. Graded for evidence quality in `13-theories.md`. |
| **[DISPUTED]** | Sources conflict, or the lore was **retconned**. See [`14`](14-retcons-and-lore-currency.md). |

## ⛔ Known gap: The Spectre

**The Spectre's origin in these documents is not reliable.** Two different accounts have appeared here and both were wrong. The current canon has not been captured, and rather than guess a third time it is marked as an open gap in [`00-the-world-and-the-spectre.md`](00-the-world-and-the-spectre.md).

Also removed as **non-canon**: the **Tyler** story — "the first victim," the prototype match against Jason/Slasher, the 200-year timeline, the naming of The Spectre — and **David**. That material is **non-canon ARG content**, was previously tagged canon here, and anchored both the timeline and a theory. All withdrawn.

## ⚠️ Old lore vs current lore

Forsaken's lore has been **rewritten repeatedly**, and retconned material is still everywhere — including in sections of the official wiki, which flags them, and in lore videos made before the changes.

The most commonly repeated dead claim: **"Azure wants to forgive Two Time but The Spectre won't let him."** That was retconned during Azure's development. Their relationship after death is now **up to interpretation**.

Three characters are under **active rewrite** and should be treated as provisional: **007n7** (whose page the wiki flags as both outdated *and* sourced from a party it no longer considers reliable), **Two Time**, and **Noli**.

Full breakdown in [`14-retcons-and-lore-currency.md`](14-retcons-and-lore-currency.md).

## The short version of the whole story

Roblox HQ had a bug. A small corruption in old, defunct server code. The bug testers — Jane Doe among them — reported it. Builderman, who ran the place, said the admins had it under control. (These events are separately sourced and appear to hold; **whether this corruption is where The Spectre came from is exactly what is in doubt** — see below.)

They did not have it under control.

⚠️ **What The Spectre is, and where it came from, is not established in these documents.** Two accounts have appeared here and both were wrong; see the gap notice above. What is documented is what it *does*: a bodiless, omnipresent entity with total authority over the world's underlying systems, which grows stronger by tormenting people — so it takes them.

It took the admins, one by one. It took whole locations out of Robloxia. Then, with the platform's moderators gone, Robloxia collapsed into the Roblox Army vs. Bacon Empire war, and The Spectre harvested that too.

Now it runs a game. It drops Survivors into stolen pieces of Robloxia and sets a Killer loose on them. It tunes everyone's abilities like a game designer balancing a patch. **How long it has been doing this is unknown** — the "two hundred years" figure that used to sit here came from the non-canon Tyler material and has been removed.

And it does not take at random:

> **[CANON]** "The Spectre takes people based on **low moments in life** and/or **high emotions and fears**, **feeding off of them**."

The wiki also calls it **"the fiend which consumes all, creating a cycle of sorrow and grief."**

So the criterion is emotional intensity and vulnerability — a person at their lowest, or at their most frightened. Guilt is the form it most often takes across this particular roster, but **fear and raw feeling qualify just as well**, and it is worth not flattening the rule into "it collects the guilty." The horror game is the harvest, and The Spectre is the one deciding it never ends.

## Important caveats

- **The lore is largely not in the game yet.** The Official Forsaken Wiki states plainly that the written lore "has not been thoroughly implemented in-game," and that the main source for character lore is **developers giving information about it** directly. Expect changes.
- **Lore has already been rewritten.** 007n7 and Two Time were originally written by a developer named Soul, who later acknowledged the characterisation was partly personal projection. After Soul left the team, Two Time was reassigned to a new writer (Toki/4Montaigne, creator of Azure) and the team publicly said both characters would be "done justice."
- **Skins are usually *not* canon.** Milestone skins are explicitly "what-if" alternate realities. Only a small number of alternate skins — notably Two Time's *Nightshade* and c00lkidd's *Mafioso* — are treated as canonical or semi-canonical.
- **Research method.** This compendium was assembled through search-engine retrieval of the Official Forsaken Wiki and secondary wikis, because direct page fetching was blocked by a network policy in the environment where it was written. Quotes are reproduced faithfully from what the sources returned, but if you are going to cite something in an argument, open the live wiki page and confirm the current wording — the wiki changes fast.
