## 1. What Forsaken actually is

**Forsaken** is an asymmetric multiplayer survival horror game released on **25 December 2024** by the **Forsaken Dev Team**, founded by **Ballsnecrosis (ninjacoola12)**. Up to **8 Survivors** must last until the round timer expires while **1 Killer** hunts them. Survivors are sorted into three classes — **Survivalist**, **Sentinel**, and **Support**.

In fiction, that gameplay loop is not a metaphor. It is literally what is happening to these characters. The rounds are real. They happen over and over. Nobody gets out.

> **[CANON]** "Forsaken takes place in a dark realm created by The Spectre, a bodyless entity using its paranormal abilities to pull in Robloxians and locations from all over Robloxia, in order to inflict further torment with its sick and twisted game."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Lore*](https://forsaken.wiki/Lore)

The cast is drawn from **real Roblox history and Roblox horror folklore**: notorious exploiters, early admins, machinima protagonists, urban legends, default avatars. Forsaken's core creative conceit is that the myths Roblox players grew up telling each other are all true, all connected, and all ended badly.

A development note worth knowing: the game was originally titled **Damnation** and was designed around *Dead by Daylight*'s loop. Roblox censored the name, and former owner **Souldrivenlove** pivoted the mechanics toward *Pillar Chase 2*. Both changes are visible in the finished product.

## 2. The Spectre

The antagonist behind everything. Not a Killer you play against — the **game master**.

### What it is

> **[CANON]** "The Spectre is a being made out of code, which seeks to obtain more power no matter the cost... a malicious omnipresent force able to shape the world, take people, and create or reshape anything to how it desires."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *The Spectre*](https://forsaken.wiki/The_Spectre)

Key established properties:

| Property | Detail |
|:--|:--|
| **Substance** | Old, defunct Roblox code. It is a living software defect. |
| **Form** | None. Bodiless, omnipresent. |
| **Domain** | Total authority over the Realm's geometry, contents, and inhabitants. |
| **Power source** | Suffering — it grows stronger by tormenting people. |
| **Reach** | Can pull both **people and places** out of Robloxia. |
| **Meta-power** | **It balances the game.** It manages and manipulates the abilities of Survivors and Killers, nerfing and buffing them at will. |

That last one deserves emphasis, because it is the single most interesting piece of worldbuilding in Forsaken. **The patch notes are diegetic.** When a character gets nerfed, in-universe, The Spectre reached in and took something away from a person. When a Killer gets buffed, a victim got a worse tormentor. The game's own live-service churn is written into the fiction as divine cruelty.

### What it wants

Power. That is the whole of it. It is not avenging anything, it has no ideology, and it is not interested in bargains. It takes people, it puts them through rounds, it eats the resulting anguish, and it gets bigger.

> **[CANON]** "The Spectre enjoys the suffering of others."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *The Spectre*](https://forsaken.wiki/The_Spectre)

### Tyler — the first victim, and the one who named it

The oldest known piece of the Realm's history, and the most disturbing.

**Tyler** was The Spectre's **first victim**. He was put up against **Jason** in a primitive prototype of what would eventually become the Forsaken game loop, and was hunted down and torn apart — repeatedly.

Then Tyler did something The Spectre had not accounted for: **he got used to it.** He grew numb to the pain. And because The Spectre's entire food supply is suffering, a victim who has stopped suffering is worse than useless.

So it punished him by taking his body away — making him formless, exactly like itself — and forced him to **watch everything that happened afterward**, forever.

It was Tyler who gave the entity the name **"The Spectre."**

> **[CANON / WMG extension]** The wiki records Tyler's numbness and his formlessness; TV Tropes' WMG page records the further claim that after roughly 200 years The Spectre eventually cast Tyler into a void to cease existing. Treat the 200-year void ending as **[THEORY]** unless you can confirm it on the wiki directly.

### David — the ARG victim

The other named early victim, surfaced through Forsaken's **ARG** material rather than the main wiki narrative.

> **[CANON — ARG]** David was described as *"far too young to be sentenced to a painful eternity"* — and The Spectre tortured him anyway, because all it wanted was more power.

David exists in the lore for exactly one reason: to establish that The Spectre has **no floor**. There is no category of victim it considers off-limits. Everything else in Forsaken's cruelty reads differently once you know it took a child and did not hesitate.

## 3. The fall of Roblox HQ

This is the origin story of everything, and it is a story about **institutional denial**.

### The sequence

1. **The corruption appears.** The Spectre begins as a small, faulty, code-like corruption inside the servers at **Roblox Headquarters** in Robloxia. At first it is tiny.
2. **The testers report it.** Bug reporters and QA staff — **Jane Doe** is named explicitly — escalate it to **Builderman** and the admin team.
3. **Builderman downplays it.** He says it will be fixed. He tells everyone the Admins have it under control. He tries to handle it alone and **stops sharing information with the rest of Roblox HQ**.
4. **It grows.** The Spectre gains more and more control over the code and files of the servers. Everyone working at HQ can see the cracks. Builderman keeps shrugging it off.
5. **It starts taking people.** Before the Roblox Army vs. Bacon Empire war, The Spectre begins kidnapping Robloxians and stealing locations — **including the admins themselves, one by one.**
6. **The Admins fall.** With the moderators gone, Robloxia has no immune system left.

> **[CANON]** "As the testers told Builderman about the issues, he shrugged them off, saying the Admins had it under control, all despite everyone working there seeing the large cracks."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Builderman*](https://forsaken.wiki/Builderman)

### The parasite, and what happened to John Doe

The corruption did not only become The Spectre. It also became **a parasite**, and the parasite took a person — which is the single most concrete thing the lore shows you about what that faulty code actually does.

**John Doe** worked a desk job at Roblox HQ. His job was **looking for faulty code**.

> **[CANON]** "Something was brewing in the old code — it had turned into **a parasite that was looking for a host**. John would be **leeched onto by the code**, with a **black substance growing on his body**. It would **tear his spine out of the back of his torso**, with **the corruption of the code keeping him undead**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *John Doe*](https://forsaken.wiki/John_Doe)

This is why Jane Doe, describing him, calls him **"John, the one with the overgrown arm."** That is what the parasite left.

And the black substance is the same thing that **grew around Jane and John's house** — the growth that got her mocked as "mold girl." The corruption followed the host home.

### Roblox HQ today

> **[CANON]** Roblox HQ "is now shown to be **completely destroyed**, being **overtaken by the parasite that infected John Doe**." It also shows that **they had been studying the parasite.**
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Roblox HQ*](https://forsaken.wiki/Roblox_HQ)

Two things worth sitting with:

1. **The building is gone**, consumed by the same growth that ate John.
2. **They were studying it.** Somebody at HQ was running research on the parasite — which sits very badly next to Builderman insisting to his staff that the Admins had it under control. Either the research was happening without him, or "under control" meant "we have a team on it," and it was not enough.

### Why this matters to the survivor roster

Because it makes the entire cast's suffering **traceable to a management failure**, and it puts the man responsible in the same rounds as his victims, with a healing kit and a guilt complex. Builderman is a Support class because he is trying to pay something back.

It also reframes at least three other survivors:

- **Jane Doe** was the one who filed the report that got ignored. Her one-sided disgust for the admins is *earned*.
- **Taph** went vigilante specifically because **the Admins fell** and unmoderated experiences were left to rot. Taph's crimes are downstream of Builderman's negligence.
- **Guest 1337's** entire war happened because, per the lore, the Bacon Empire declared war on Guests *after* the admins disappeared.

### The Roblox HQ staff

Named HQ-era figures: **Builderman** (leader), **Shedletsky** (creative director), **Dusekkar**, **MrDoomBringer / Doombringer**, and **John and Jane Doe**. Most are now in the Realm.

## 4. The Collapse and the Bacon War

Two connected catastrophes.

**The Bacon Empire war.** Following the disappearance of the admins, the **Bacon Empire** — led by the **Bacon General** — declared war on Guests and anyone supporting them, escalating to an all-out attempt to seize control of all of Roblox by force. This is the war **Guest 1337** enlisted in after the genocide of his people, and it is drawn directly from **ObliviousHD's *The Last Guest*** machinima series.

**The Collapse of Robloxia.** Secondary sources describe The Spectre as responsible for *"the first successful Collapse of Robloxia, a mass genocide that supposedly caused the extinction of the Robloxian race."*

> **[SECONDARY]** The "Collapse / extinction of the Robloxian race" framing comes from the Villains Wiki entry on The Spectre rather than `forsaken.wiki`. It is consistent with the official material but is the strongest version of the claim. Flagged accordingly.

If accurate, it reframes the Realm entirely: it is not a prison carved out of a living world. It may be **most of what's left**.

## 5. The Realm itself

### Geography

The maps are **not invented**. They are **stolen**. The Spectre pulls real locations out of Robloxia and installs them in its Realm, which is why survivors are hunted through places they used to live and work — **Builder Brothers Pizza**, **Sword Fights on the Heights**, the Spawn Cult's village.

This is a targeted cruelty. Elliot is chased through his own workplace. Shedletsky fights for his life in the arena he designed.

### The Lobby

Between rounds, the cast occupies a **Lobby**, which functions as the social hub and the game's main lore-delivery space. It contains:

- **Lobby NPCs**, including **Jane Doe** in the **Cabin basement**, who acts as the Realm's **lore keeper** and hands out the document quests.
- A **corkboard** showing the connections between Guest 1337, Noob, Shedletsky, Builderman, 007n7, Taph, Elliot and Dusekkar — an in-universe relationship map.
- **Milestone NPCs**, which appear as you level individual characters.

### Last Man Standing

When one Survivor remains, **Last Man Standing** triggers: a **1:14** timer, a music change, an LMS portrait flash, and a mutual 6-second highlight between the last survivor and the Killer.

In-universe this is The Spectre **turning up the theatre** for the finale — and some Killer skins have custom LMS themes that alter the timer or effects, meaning the entity personalises the ending.

### The scrapyard

Jane Doe's entry point. She describes arriving in a **"scrapyard"** that functioned as a **waiting room** — a holding area before proper entry into the Realm. It was there, unnoticed, that she wrote her three documents.

### Helperbot / D3functB0t

A robot built to assist and inspire Roblox creators, dragged into the Realm and nicknamed **D3functB0t**. **Builderman is slowly repairing it using parts stripped from generators** — the same generators the Survivors have to repair each round.

It is a small, devastating detail. The man who broke the world is spending his eternity rebuilding one small broken machine out of scavenged parts, because it is the only thing left he can actually fix.

## 6. How The Spectre chooses

Not random. There is a selection criterion, and it is the thesis statement of the entire roster.

> **[DEV / Word of God]** The Spectre focuses on gathering Killers and Survivors who possess **negative feelings — guilt and grief** — and personally favours those who are **extremely emotional**.

Run the roster against that filter:

| Survivor | The guilt or grief |
|:--|:--|
| **Noob** | Believes they caused their best friend's disappearance |
| **Shedletsky** | Created 1x1x1x1; hides it from everyone |
| **Elliot** | Chronic self-sacrifice; family loss |
| **007n7** | His past victims, and his lost son |
| **Guest 1337** | Murdered parents, genocide of his people, his own death |
| **Two Time** | Murdered the person who loved them |
| **Taph** | Killed sentient NPCs while "helping" |
| **Chance** | Burned every relationship he had; was murdered |
| **Builderman** | Let The Spectre exist |
| **Dusekkar** | Failed to save Shedletsky; keeps the secret |
| **Jane Doe** | Lost her husband; blamed for it |
| **Veeronica** | Vandalised instead of helping |

Twelve for twelve. **Forsaken's survivors are not selected for being good people in a bad situation. They are selected for being haunted.** The Realm is not arbitrary — it is a collection of people specifically chosen because they were already punishing themselves, put somewhere the punishment never ends.

### The big unanswered question

> **[CANON]** How most of the Survivors were forsaken **has not been explained**.

The documented exceptions:

- **Dusekkar** — taken **in his sleep, along with his entire house**.
- **Guest 1337** — **died** detonating a grenade to kill the Bacon General, then was taken.
- **Elliot** — Forsaken during a **scooter accident**.
- **Jane Doe** — **walked in voluntarily**, following her husband's trail.
- **Chance** — **murdered** by iTrapped, then taken "while it's currently unclear how."
- **Two Time** — strongly implied to have **died before** being Forsaken.

Note the pattern forming: several survivors appear to have **died first**. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 1.

## 7. Artifacts and factions

### The Necrobloxicon

A **cursed, demonic book** of unknown and extremely old origin. Full of ancient text, demonic rituals, and writings that drive readers mad. Critically, it grants **access to the Lua code that the Roblox world runs on**.

It is a parody of H.P. Lovecraft's *Necronomicon*, based on the limited Roblox accessory, and it plays a pivotal role in **the Chronicles of Roblox**, the lore series written by **MrDoombringer and Shedletsky** in real life.

Two confirmed effects:

1. **Dracula** found it and used it to create powerful vampire servants.
2. **Telamon's incidental exposure to it** caused him to physically separate from his own hatred — creating **1x1x1x1**.

It appears in-game attached to **Slasher's** right shoulder.

That Shedletsky co-wrote the real Chronicles of Roblox, and that the artifact from that series is what destroys his character in Forsaken, is a genuinely elegant piece of metafiction.

### The Family Home for The Reborn (the Spawn Cult)

Led by **Elder Amarah**. Worships **"The Spawn"** — a **fake** son of **2x2**, who is a genuine god responsible for the creation of the world. The doctrine promises that sufficient devotion grants **the ability to respawn**.

It is a lie built on a real god, and underneath it the cult is doing something much worse: the "chosen one" feast is a **disguised cannibalistic dinner**, where a dead Robloxian from the church cellar is butchered, cooked, and served to members who have no idea what they are eating.

This is **Two Time's** origin and **Azure's** death. See [`06-two-time.md`](06-two-time.md).

### The Banlands

Where Roblox Admins sentence caught exploiters. **Ellernate** and **Caleb244** were sent there; their friend **iTrapped** escaped capture and spent his life trying to get them out — which is what got **Chance** killed. See [`08-chance.md`](08-chance.md).

### Phosphorus

An entity described as a **direct agent of The Spectre**. The **Phosphorus symbol** appears in multiple character designs, and is the anchor of one of the fandom's best-supported theories. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 3.

## 8. The timeline

Approximate and partly conjectural, since the wiki gives few hard dates.

| Era | Events |
|:--|:--|
| **Foundation** | Builderman and Dusekkar are among the first Robloxians. Robloxia is built "brick by brick." Shedletsky is recruited as creative director. |
| **Golden age** | *Sword Fights on the Heights* flourishes. Telamon becomes a legend. Shedletsky marries BrightEyes. HQ runs: Builderman, Shedletsky, Dusekkar, Doombringer, John & Jane Doe. |
| **The Telamon incident** | Telamon's ego peaks. An unknown "utterly selfish and deplorable act," plus Necrobloxicon exposure, splits his hatred off as **1x1x1x1**. |
| **The corruption** | The Spectre forms in HQ's servers. Jane Doe and other testers report it. Builderman denies it. |
| **Tyler** | The Spectre's first victim, vs. Jason, in a prototype Realm. **At least 200 years before the present cast.** |
| **The admin abductions** | The Spectre takes admins and locations one by one. The Admins fall. |
| **The Bacon War** | Bacon Empire declares war on Guests. Guest 1337 fights. The Bacon General dies. Guest 1337 dies with him. |
| **The unmoderated era** | With admins gone, Taph begins demolishing unmoderated experiences. Exploiters run unchecked. |
| **The disappearances** | c00lkidd vanishes. John Doe vanishes. The March 18th rumours spread. |
| **The present** | Twelve Survivors, an expanding Killer roster, endless rounds. |

> **Note on Tyler's 200 years.** The wiki says it was *"at least 200 years since Tyler's story"* when the rest of the cast arrived. That is a very long time for an entity that started as a server bug — and it implies the Realm predates most of the events above by centuries, which does not sit comfortably alongside The Spectre originating at Roblox HQ. This is an open inconsistency in the lore, not a solved puzzle.

## 9. How the lore is delivered

Worth understanding before you argue about canon with anyone.

> **[CANON]** "Currently, the lore written has not been thoroughly implemented in-game, and it is planned to be added in-game being told through different means, however currently the main source for the lore behind characters are through **Developers giving information about it**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Lore*](https://forsaken.wiki/Lore)

So Forsaken's lore lives across five channels:

1. **Developer statements** — Discord, X. The primary source. Also the least archived.
2. **Jane Doe's document quests** — the main in-game delivery mechanism. Three quests: *"Aim for the Top"* (Shedletsky's document), *"Once Was No Longer Is"* (Dusekkar's), *"Fundamental Principle of Inertia"* (Builderman's).
3. **The ARG** — source for Tyler, David, and the encoded Guest 666 letters.
4. **Skins, milestones and voicelines** — mostly "what-if," occasionally canon.
5. **Lobby NPCs and environmental detail** — the corkboard, Helperbot, the scrapyard.

### Canon hazards

- **Milestone skins are explicitly alternate realities**, not canon.
- **Exceptions exist**: Two Time's **Nightshade** is confirmed canonical; **Mafioso** is semi-canonical and pivotal to Chance's backstory; Two Time's **milestones** are also described as canon by secondary sources.
- **Retcons happen.** Azure's rare voicelines once called Two Time **"Thomas."** Retconned.
- **Writers change.** 007n7 and Two Time were written by **Soul**, who acknowledged projecting personally onto them. After Soul left, Two Time went to **Toki/4Montaigne**, and the team said both characters would be "done justice." Any 007n7 or Two Time lore predating that handover is **provisional**.
- **Real people are involved.** The person behind the **007n7 and c00lkidd** accounts **worked with the team** on that character's lore. The real **John Shedletsky** has publicly reposted Forsaken content.
