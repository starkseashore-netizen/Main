## 1. Who Two Time is

The darkest survivor on the roster, and the only one whose backstory is a **premeditated murder of a loved one**.

> **[CANON]** "Two Time bears **nothing other than a sleek dagger and manic smile**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Two Time*](https://forsaken.wiki/Two_Time)

A dagger and a manic smile. That's the character.

But Two Time is not written as a villain, and this is what makes them the best-constructed tragedy in the game: they are a **groomed child who was manipulated into killing**, by an adult who had raised them, inside a cult that was lying about everything.

### ⚠️ Lore status warning

Two Time's lore was originally written by **Soul**, who acknowledged that **key parts of the characterisation were based on personal projection**. After Soul left, Two Time was **reassigned to Toki / 4Montaigne** — the creator of **Azure** — and the team publicly stated the character would be **"done justice."**

Two Time is therefore the survivor whose lore has been **most actively revised**. The fact that the writer of their victim now writes them is significant: the two characters are being developed as one story.

**[DISPUTED]** A retcon is already documented: in rare Azure voicelines, Two Time was referred to as **"Thomas."** This has since been **retconned**.

## 2. The cult

You cannot understand Two Time without understanding what they were raised inside.

### What it claims to be

> **[CANON]** "The **Family Home for the Reborn**, also known commonly as the **Spawn Cult**, is a religion inside of the world of Forsaken, led by **Elder Amarah**. The cult would host sermons for **'The Spawn'**, a **fake son of 2x2**, who is an **actual god** responsible for the creation of the world."

The theology is a forgery built on a real foundation:

- **2x2 is real.** A genuine god, responsible for creating the world.
- **"The Spawn" is not.** A fabricated son, invented by the cult.

That's what makes it work. Members aren't believing in nothing — they're believing in a **counterfeit heir to a genuine throne**.

> **[CANON]** Members "believe that with enough **devotion** shown towards the Spawn, they will achieve **the ability to respawn**."

### What it actually is

> **[CANON]** "The **chosen one** and **feast** hosted by the villagers is a **disguised cannibalistic dinner**, where a **dead Robloxian coming from the cellar of the church** was chosen out and was **cut up and cooked** for the other members to eat, **oblivious to what they're eating**."

The cult murders people, stores the bodies in the church cellar, and **serves them to its own congregation** under the guise of a religious feast. The rank-and-file don't know. Only the elders do.

**[SECONDARY]** Community analysis of in-game clues around the cannibalism is an active discussion thread in the Forsaken fandom — and it is one of the theories that turned out to be **confirmed by the official wiki**.

## 3. Life before the Realm

### The outsider

> **[CANON]** "Two Time was once **carried by Elder Amarah**, the cult's leader, a long time ago when Two Time was a child, **viewing Amarah as a parental figure**."

Amarah — the head of the cannibal cult — **raised them**. That is the relationship. Everything that follows is a parent figure using a child.

> **[CANON]** "Two Time was **ostracized from the other members**, being **excluded from the cult's rituals and meetings** and weren't close with the other cultists, **always given weird looks**."

So Two Time's position was uniquely awful: raised by the leader, rejected by everyone else. Inside enough to have no other home; outside enough to be desperate to earn a place. **That is a textbook grooming setup**, and the lore knows it.

### Aspen

> **[CANON]** "When Two Time was **fetching lumber** for the cult, another member named **Azure** would be **scared and mistake them for a beast**, before realizing the situation. The two would socialize and introduce each other, with **Azure giving them the Two Time nickname**."

The meeting is almost funny — someone mistook them for a monster in the woods, then apologised.

Note: **"Two Time" is not their name.** It is a **nickname Aspen gave them.** Their original name is unknown (the "Thomas" voiceline is retconned). Meanwhile "Aspen" is *his* real name; **"Azure"** is what he becomes afterwards.

> **[CANON]** "They went back to the village, and became close friends — **studying the cult's bible** and **attempting rituals and prayings in several flower fields** that were in the forest."

**[SECONDARY]** "Azure and Two Time were **canonically dating** before Forsaken."

So: the only person who ever treated Two Time as a person. Who named them. Who studied with them, prayed with them in flower fields, and loved them.

**Remember the flower fields.** They come back.

## 4. The murder

### What Aspen found

> **[CANON]** "**Aspen grew suspicious of the elders** of the Reborn, seeing them **sneak into the basement before feasts**. Eventually, his curiosity would get the best of them and he would **enter the basement one night** while everyone was asleep, where he would find **numerous bodies of people who were killed by the cult**."

**[CANON]** Elsewhere described as "a storage room deep inside one of the village's buildings, **full of killed people hung in bags, with chunks of their bodies missing** and blood everywhere."

Chunks missing. Because they'd already been served.

### The confrontation

> **[CANON]** "Azure would later **confront Two Time about it, begging them to leave together**, and Two Time would **initially agree** — however **Elder Amarah would be able to manipulate them otherwise**."

This is the hinge of the entire character, and it has to be read carefully:

1. Aspen found the truth.
2. He came to Two Time first — not to the authorities, **to them** — and asked them to run away together.
3. **Two Time said yes.**
4. Then Amarah — the person who had raised them — got to them, and turned them.

**Two Time chose correctly first.** Given the truth and an escape and the person they loved, they agreed to go. It took the direct intervention of their parent figure to undo it.

### The field

> **[CANON]** "**Aspen was waiting for Two Time in the forest where they always met up**, where Two Time would **attack Aspen and stab them in the chest with their dagger, killing him**."

He was waiting in their spot. The flower field where they used to pray together. He thought they were coming to run away with him.

**[SECONDARY]** "Two Time killed Azure **in a field of Nightshades**, which is **what the cult's bible had intended**."

So the murder was **scripted by doctrine**. The flower field where they prayed was, per the bible they studied together, the prescribed altar. Their entire relationship had been taking place inside the geometry of a ritual neither of them understood.

Motive, per the wiki: Two Time killed him to **receive their second life** — the respawn the cult promised in exchange for devotion, via Robloxian sacrifice.

### The witness

> **[CANON]** "**Investigative Iris witnessed this**, where she would be **thrown off the cliffside by an unknown individual**. What happened to Two Time and Azure in this moment is **unknown** — however, the individual responsible for killing Iris would **transform Aspen into Azure**, a **rotted corpse puppeteered by a hat controlled by The Spectre**."

A **third party** was present. Someone who:

- Murdered a witness,
- Then reanimated Aspen's corpse as **Azure**,
- With a **hat that The Spectre controls**.

That is an **agent of The Spectre, physically present at the murder.** Not observing from outside — *there*, cleaning up and harvesting.

Which raises the real question: **was any of this Two Time's idea?** A cult whose doctrine prescribes murder in a specific flower field, an elder who turns them at the exact moment they try to leave, and an operative standing by to collect the body. The wiki says what happened to Two Time in that moment is **unknown**. That gap is deliberate.

## 5. How Two Time was Forsaken

Not explicitly stated — but the strongest surviving hint is a skin.

> **[SECONDARY]** "**Nightshade Two Time** is the **only alternative skin that has been confirmed canonical** to the lore, and it might have to do with **them dying before being forsaken by Spectre**."
> **[SECONDARY]** "The Nightshade skin is based on Azure, whose **favourite flower is the nightshade**, and could be a reference to the fictional **hanahaki disease**, which involves **flowers growing in the body as a result of unrequited love**. It's theorised this was the state Two Time was in **right before being forsaken**."

Assemble it:

- The murder happened in a **nightshade field**.
- Nightshade was **Aspen's favourite flower**.
- Nightshade is **poisonous**.
- The **Nightshade skin is canon** — one of the only ones.
- Hanahaki: **flowers growing inside the body from unrequited love**.

The reading: after killing him, Two Time **died of it** — poisoned, or grief-stricken, or both, in the field where they killed him, with his favourite flower growing out of them. Then The Spectre took what was left.

If so, Two Time joins **Guest 1337**, **Chance**, and **Azure** as survivors who were **dead before they arrived**.

## 6. In the Realm — and Azure

The cruellest arrangement in Forsaken.

**Azure** is a **Killer**. He is Aspen's rotted corpse, **puppeteered by a hat The Spectre controls**, resurrected specifically to be **preyed on for his negative emotions** and turned into a monster.

> **[CANON]** "Azure is a former member of the Family Home for The Reborn, **killed by their partner in a ritualistic sacrifice** after learning the truth. After their death, they were **resurrected by The Spectre** and **preyed on for their negative emotions**, **now controlled by their hat**, turning into a monster."

And then:

> **[SECONDARY]** "It is **canon that Azure wants to forgive Two Time** — **but the Spectre does not allow him to.**"

Read that one more time.

He **wants to forgive them.** After being lied to, murdered in a flower field by the person he loved, and reanimated as a puppet — **he still wants to forgive them.** And The Spectre, which is wearing him like a glove, **will not permit it**, because reconciliation would end the suffering it feeds on.

The Spectre is not making Azure hunt Two Time out of rage. **It is overriding forgiveness that is already there.** Every round they meet, Two Time faces someone who has already pardoned them and cannot say so.

**[THEORY]** The Seven Deadly Sins reading assigns **Azure** — full name given as **Azurewrath** — the sin of **Wrath**, "with his hatred for Two Time clouding his proper judgement." Note the friction: the wrath is The Spectre's imposition, not Azure's own state. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 3.

## 7. The kit as characterisation

**Sentinel** — and the most thematically literal kit in the game.

| Ability | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| **Sacrificial Dagger** | Stun the Killer by **backstabbing** them, fuelling the **Oblation** bar |
| **Ritual point** | With a full Oblation bar, Two Time **respawns** at a marked point |

Every word is doctrine:

- **"Sacrificial Dagger"** — the literal murder weapon.
- **"Oblation"** — a religious offering.
- **"Ritual point"** — the altar.
- **The mechanic: stab someone in the back, and you get to come back to life.**

Two Time's entire gameplay is **re-performing the murder of Aspen, over and over, to earn the respawn the cult promised.** They stab someone in the back, fill the offering bar, and are reborn. That is exactly the deal they were sold — and in the Realm, **it actually works.**

The Spectre has given them functioning proof of a religion that was a lie. Their sin now pays out, mechanically, every round. The one thing they murdered for is the one thing they get.

And the Killer they're most likely to be backstabbing is Azure.

## 9. The story, assembled

> The leader of the cult carried them as a child, and everyone else in the village looked at them strangely and left them out. So they grew up belonging to one person and nobody else, which is a very useful way for a child to be raised if you intend to use them later.
>
> Then somebody mistook them for a monster in the woods, realised his error, apologised, and became the first friend they ever had. He gave them a nickname. They went back together and studied the cult's bible, and prayed in the flower fields, and fell in love.
>
> And Aspen started noticing that the elders went down to the basement before every feast.
>
> One night he went down there himself and found the bodies hanging in bags with pieces cut out of them, and understood what the village had been eating.
>
> He went to Two Time. Not to the law — to *them*. He told them everything and begged them to run away together.
>
> **They said yes.**
>
> Then Elder Amarah got to them. And whatever was said, it was enough.
>
> Aspen waited in the flower field where they always met, because he thought they were coming to escape with him. They came with a dagger. The cult's bible had specified a field of nightshades, and that is where he died, and the reason was a second life that the cult had made up.
>
> Somebody else was watching. She was thrown off a cliff. And whoever threw her stood over Aspen's body and put a hat on it, and the hat belonged to The Spectre, and Aspen got up again as something else.
>
> What happened to Two Time in that field is not recorded. But their only canon outfit is covered in nightshade — his favourite flower, and a poison — and the story it tells is of flowers growing out of a body in a field, from love that had nowhere left to go.
>
> Both of them ended up here.
>
> He is a Killer now, a corpse worn like a puppet, kept angry by the thing inside him. And underneath that, he **wants to forgive them** — he has wanted to for a long time — and the thing wearing him will not let him say it.
>
> And they are a Survivor, with a dagger and a manic smile, stabbing things in the back to fill an offering bar so they can come back to life.
>
> The religion was fake. The respawn was a lie. But down here, it works. It works every single round.
>
> They got exactly what they killed him for.

## 10. Open questions

1. **Who killed Investigative Iris and raised Azure?** An agent of The Spectre, present at the murder, unidentified. This is one of the biggest open threads in the game.
2. **How was Two Time Forsaken?** Nightshade implies death, but the wiki doesn't state it.
3. **What is their real name?** "Two Time" is Aspen's nickname; "Thomas" was retconned.
4. **What did Amarah say?** The exact manipulation that flipped them is unrecorded — and is arguably the true moral crux of the character.
5. **What became of the cult?** Elder Amarah's fate is undocumented.
6. **How much survives the rewrite?** Two Time is the most actively revised character in Forsaken.
7. **Will Azure ever get to forgive them?** That is, presumably, the endgame — and the reason The Spectre will never allow it.
