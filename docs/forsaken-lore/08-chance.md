## 1. Who Chance is

The gambler. Charming, reckless, catastrophically lucky — and the survivor with the clearest documented **murder** in his file.

> **[CANON]** "Chance is a **gentleperson gambler**, who used to have various connections through investing and companies. Chance would recklessly spend their hard earned cash from his company connections and trading through gambling, due to the fact that Chance had **just one goal in life: to live out life no matter what dangers and risks he had to take**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Chance*](https://forsaken.wiki/Chance)

That goal is worth sitting with. Chance isn't greedy. He isn't chasing money — he **had** money, and threw it away on purpose. What he wanted was **to feel alive**, and the only reliable way he knew was to put something at risk.

> **[CANON]** "Being born as **both a thrill seeker and an investor**."

Those two things do not belong in the same person, and that contradiction is Chance.

---

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The reputation

> **[CANON]** "Chance became a **famous business man** and **infamous gambler** for his **high luck**."

Famous for one, infamous for the other. He was respected in business and notorious at the tables, and the thing that made him notorious was **winning too much.**

Hold onto that. It is what kills him.

### The isolation

> **[CANON]** "However, due to their **reckless behavior**, it caused **many people to push them away**, **except for someone named iTrapped**."

This is the real tragedy of Chance, and it's easy to miss under the flash. He gambled away his money, and with it **every relationship he had**. By the end, out of everyone he knew — business partners, investors, friends — **exactly one person was still around.**

And that person was there on purpose.

### iTrapped

> **[CANON]** "**iTrapped is an exploiter** who formed a hacking group with his friends **Ellernate** and **Caleb244**. After Ellernate and Caleb244 were **caught by Roblox Admins and sentenced to the Banlands**, iTrapped **managed to avoid capture**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *iTrapped*](https://forsaken.wiki/ITrapped)

> **[CANON]** "Determined to find a way to get his friends out of the Banlands, iTrapped **lessened his exploiting** and turned to the **black market and other corporations to grow in wealth**, eventually meeting **Chance**... iTrapped wanted to **manipulate Chance's company connections** so he could **get his banned friends out of the Banlands**."

iTrapped is not a simple villain. He is a man who watched his friends get sentenced, escaped himself, gave up the thing that got them caught, and spent his life trying to break them out.

He is, in other words, **loyal** — and it is precisely his loyalty that makes him lethal. He befriended the loneliest rich man in Robloxia because that man had the connections he needed.

**The symmetry is brutal.** Chance's only remaining friend was cultivating him. And iTrapped's motive — *I will do anything for the people I love* — is the exact virtue Chance never had, aimed directly at him.

### The casino

> **[CANON]** "Chance one day would go into a casino owned by a man named **Don Sonnellino**, better known as **Mafioso**, who is the **leader of a mafia group**, **scamming money out of the people unfortunate enough to fall for their scams**."

A rigged house. Fixed games. An operation built on the certainty that nobody beats it.

> **[CANON]** "Chance would later enter the casino and gamble. **Despite all the rigged odds he won.** Mafioso **refuses to believe he won legitimately**, and **hunts him down to reclaim his goods**."

**[SECONDARY]** Elsewhere described as **"a rigged game that was meant to be impossible."**

Chance did not cheat. That's the point, and it's what makes it funny and then horrible. **His only actual crime was being lucky.** He walked into a scam designed so that winning was impossible, and won, because being impossibly lucky is literally what he is.

And Mafioso — a professional criminal whose entire business model depends on the games being unbeatable — **could not accept it.** Not because Chance cheated, but because the alternative was admitting the house could lose.

So Chance took the prize money and ran, and a mafia went after him.

**Note:** Mafioso exists in-game as a **c00lkidd skin**, and is **[SECONDARY]** one of the few **semi-canonical** skins in Forsaken and **one of the few characters not forsaken by The Spectre.** He is still out there.

### The murder

> **[CANON]** "One night, **iTrapped killed Chance with a stolen Darkheart sword** and then **ran away to begin his plan to save his friends**."

There it is, cleanly stated: **Chance was murdered.**

Details that matter:

- **The weapon was stolen** — a **Darkheart**, one of Roblox's legendary limited swords. iTrapped didn't own it; he took it for this.
- **It happened after** iTrapped had "obtained everything he needed." This was the **last step of a plan**, not a fight.
- **He ran straight to the rescue.** He didn't linger, didn't loot. He killed Chance and left to free Ellernate and Caleb244.

So Chance's death was an **errand**. Not revenge, not passion, not even hatred — an item on a checklist, executed by the only person who still talked to him, on the way to doing something genuinely good for people who deserved it more.

**And the mafia never got him.** Mafioso hunted him across Robloxia, and it was the friend at his elbow who actually did it.

## 3. How Chance was Forsaken

> **[CANON]** "Chance was later **taken into The Spectre's realm** and became Forsaken himself, though **it's currently unclear how**."

The wiki's sequencing is notable: **murdered first, taken second.** It does not say the murder *is* the abduction — it says the abduction is unclear — but the order is on the page.

**[THEORY]** A widely-circulated community version claims Chance was convinced by iTrapped to play **Buckshot Roulette**, won, was stabbed in a hallway, and was **dragged into the Realm by The Spectre while still slightly alive.** The "still slightly alive" detail is the interesting one — it would make Chance the only survivor taken **mid-death** rather than before or after. Not canon. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 1.

## 4. In the Realm

A man whose entire identity was built on **risk**, dropped into a place where he is risked **constantly, against his will, forever.**

This is the most precisely-targeted punishment on the roster. Chance's one goal in life was to live it "no matter what dangers and risks he had to take." The Spectre gave him infinite danger — and by making it **mandatory**, took away the only thing that made risk worth anything to him: **choosing it.**

A gambler who cannot leave the table is not a gambler. He's just losing.

---

## 5. The kit as characterisation

**Sentinel.** And uniquely, a Sentinel who has to **ask permission** from the universe before he's allowed to help.

> **[CANON]** "His playstyle is centered around **constant gambling and luck**, using his **Coin Flip** to gain favor and either **help himself or interrupt the Killer**."

| Ability | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| **Coin Flip** | Gamble for "favor." Depending on the result, either **helps Chance** or **interrupts the Killer** |

The design says everything:

1. **His power is literally a coin toss.** Every other survivor has a tool that does a thing. Chance has *odds*.
2. **He never stopped gambling.** He's in eternal hell and his method of survival is still **flipping a coin and hoping.** He hasn't learned anything. He can't.
3. **"Favor" is the word.** Not charge, not energy — **favor**. Something granted from outside, by something that may or may not like you.

And who controls outcomes in the Realm? **The Spectre**, which "manages and manipulates the abilities of the survivors and killers."

Which means every time Chance flips that coin, he is gambling **against the entity that owns the game, in a house it built and rigged.**

He's done this before. He won that time. It got him killed.

**The $777 price tag** is the last joke: three sevens, the jackpot, for the character whose defining trait is winning when he shouldn't.

## 7. Relationships

| Character | Relationship |
|:--|:--|
| **iTrapped** | His only remaining friend. Cultivated him for his connections. **Murdered him with a stolen Darkheart.** |
| **Don Sonnellino / Mafioso** | Mafia boss whose rigged casino Chance beat. Hunted him for it. **Never caught him.** Not forsaken. |
| **Ellernate & Caleb244** | iTrapped's friends in the **Banlands**. The reason Chance died. They have never met him. |
| **Everyone else** | Pushed away by his recklessness. |

Chance has **no relationships inside the Realm** documented in his lore. Every person who mattered to him is outside it: one is a murderer, one is a mafia boss, two are prisoners who don't know his name.

He is the roster's most socially isolated survivor — which, given that his defining trait is charm, is the cruellest possible outcome.

## 8. The story, assembled

> He had money and connections and a good name in business, and he spent all of it at the tables, because the only time he felt properly alive was when something was at stake.
>
> He was extraordinarily lucky. That's not a figure of speech — it's the thing about him. He won when he shouldn't, consistently enough that it became his reputation.
>
> And he burned through every person he knew doing it. One by one they got tired and left, until there was exactly one friend left standing next to him.
>
> That friend was called iTrapped, and he was there because he needed something.
>
> iTrapped had run with two others, Ellernate and Caleb244, until the admins caught them and sent them to the Banlands. He got away. And he decided he was going to get them out, whatever it took — which meant money, and contacts, and a way into the kind of corporate connections a famous investor might have.
>
> So he found the loneliest lucky man in Robloxia and became his only friend.
>
> Somewhere in there, Chance walked into a casino run by Don Sonnellino, who ran his tables rigged and his games impossible, and took money off people who never stood a chance. Chance played. Chance won.
>
> He didn't cheat. He was just lucky, the way he always was. But a man who sells the certainty that the house can't lose cannot allow it to have happened, so Sonnellino decided Chance had stolen from him and set his people hunting.
>
> Chance took the winnings and ran, and the mafia came after him, and that should have been the end of the story.
>
> It wasn't. The mafia never got him.
>
> One night iTrapped — who by then had everything he needed — killed him with a Darkheart he'd stolen for the purpose, and then left immediately to go free his friends. Chance wasn't an enemy. He was a step.
>
> Then something took what was left. The record says it isn't clear how.
>
> So now he's here, in a place where he is gambled with every single round, forever, and he never chose a single one of those bets.
>
> And his answer to all of it is to reach into his pocket and flip a coin and hope the house is feeling generous. He's playing a rigged game against something that cannot lose.
>
> He did that once before. He won.
>
> That's what got him killed.

## 9. Open questions

1. **How was Chance Forsaken?** "Currently unclear." Murdered first, taken after.
2. **Did iTrapped free Ellernate and Caleb244?** He ran off to do it. No record of whether it worked, which means Chance may have died for nothing.
3. **Where is iTrapped now?** Not a Killer, not a Survivor. Still out there, as far as anyone knows.
4. **Whose Darkheart was it?** "Stolen" — from whom is never said.
5. **Does Mafioso know Chance is dead?** He is one of the few not forsaken; presumably still hunting a man who no longer exists.
6. **Does Chance know who killed him?** Nothing suggests he understands the betrayal.
