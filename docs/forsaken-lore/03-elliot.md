## 1. Who Elliot is

The healer. The one who throws pizza at your feet while you're bleeding out and then runs back into danger. He is a free starter character and, mechanically, the friendliest possible introduction to Support play.

> **[CANON]** "Elliot is deeply kind, **always overworking himself**. Elliot makes sure to always help people first before him, **even if it gets him hurt**."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Elliot*](https://forsaken.wiki/Elliot)

Read that as a character flaw, because that is how it's written. Elliot's kindness is **compulsive**, not chosen. It is the behaviour of someone who has decided his own wellbeing is the last item on the list, and the lore is careful to show where that habit came from.

He is also — and this is where Elliot gets genuinely strange — the subject of the fandom's most persistent "this character is not what he seems" theory, and unusually, the theory has real evidence behind it. See §7.

### Real-world basis

**Elliot Builder** is the pizza delivery boy mascot of **Work at a Pizza Place**, one of the longest-running and most beloved games on Roblox — and, not coincidentally, one of the most frequently *destroyed* by exploiters. Forsaken takes the platform's most wholesome workplace and makes it a crime scene.

## 2. Life before the Realm

### The family

> **[CANON]** "Elliot Builder was **born into a wealthy family**, being one of the sons for the **Builder Brothers**, better known for owning the successful **Builder Brothers Pizza** restaurant."

But the wealth did not translate into the life you'd expect:

> **[CANON]** "Elliot **lived modestly**, with his little sister **Mia**, **Mr. Builder**, and an unnamed mother figure. Unfortunately, **the mother would pass away**, leaving Elliot and Mr. Builder to take care of the family."

So: money in the family, but a modest life. A dead mother. A younger sister to look after. And a father to share the load with.

**This is where the overworking comes from.** Elliot learned, young, that if he did not carry things, the people around him would drop.

**[SECONDARY]** The Fandom wiki adds a telling motive: Elliot worked every role in the restaurant **to build his own wealth rather than rely on his father's financial success.** He wasn't obligated to work. He chose to, and then chose to do all of it.

### The job

> **[CANON]** "As he became older, Elliot earned a job at Builder Brothers Pizza, being **the only employee to stay the longest** in the restaurant. He worked in **delivering pizzas across the island**, and taking spots in the workplace when understaffed."

**[SECONDARY]** TV Tropes: he worked every position and **remained employee of the month for an indefinite period** — and he *continues doing chores even after being forsaken.*

That last detail is the most quietly heartbreaking thing in Elliot's file. He is in hell. He is still doing his shifts.

### The exploiters

Here Elliot's story collides with two other survivors' — and this is Forsaken's tightest bit of interlocking continuity.

> **[CANON]** "Elliot frequently had to endure the pizzeria being destroyed and targeted by various exploiters, **notoriously by 007n7 and c00lkidd**, who both got on a ban list after the countless property damage to the restaurant."

**[SECONDARY]** The specific incident, per the Fandom wiki: **c00lkidd — the 10-year-old adopted son of 007n7 — fired a rocket launcher at Elliot while he was working the cashier.** Both were subsequently blacklisted and permanently banned.

So the roster contains:

- **Elliot**, who was attacked at work.
- **007n7**, the man who used to wreck that same pizzeria, who now has to stand next to him every round.
- **c00lkidd**, the child who shot a rocket at him, now a **Killer** who hunts him.

> **[SECONDARY]** 007n7 "**feels guilt over his past actions as seen in his interactions with Elliot and Builderman.**"

The Realm forced an apology dynamic that the real world never would have.

## 3. How Elliot was Forsaken

One of the few explicitly documented abductions:

> **[CANON]** "Elliot was Forsaken while using a **scooter from Builder Brothers Pizza**, getting into an **accident**. It was **bent and broken out-of-shape**, and he's been working on trying to **get it fixed ever since in his free time**."

Several things here:

1. **He was on the job.** Forsaken mid-delivery. Working, as always.
2. **The scooter came with him.** The Spectre takes objects and places, not just people.
3. **He is still repairing it.** In his free time. In hell.

That scooter is Elliot's entire psychology in one prop. It is broken, it is not going to take him home, and he works on it anyway — because fixing things and looking after people is the only mode he has.

**[THEORY]** A darker community reading holds that Elliot's fate was **written for him** — that he was "burnt and crushed by the pizza place" as a deliberate punishment. Non-canon. See §7.

## 5. The kit as characterisation

**Support** — healing, enabling, keeping other people upright.

| Ability | Effect |
|:--|:--|
| **Pizza Throw** | Tosses a pizza that instantly heals another Survivor **5 HP**, then heals **15 HP over 4s** to whoever picks it up |
| **Rush Hour** | Gains a charge whenever Elliot heals anyone. Starts with 1, stores up to 3. |

Look at the design:

- **Pizza Throw heals *other survivors*.** He cannot eat it himself. His entire primary ability is **incapable of helping him**.
- **Rush Hour only charges when he heals someone else.** His personal power *requires* him to be useful to other people first. He cannot get stronger except by serving.

The mechanics literally encode "helps people first before him, even if it gets him hurt." And it's called **Rush Hour** — his reward for caring for people is *a busier shift.*

## 6. Skins as lore

Elliot's cosmetics carry more lore weight than most, largely because of Theory 4.

| Skin | Detail |
|:--|:--|
| **Messenger** | **Winged visor**, orange and white robes, wristbands, brown satchel. The anchor of the Spectre-connection theory. |
| **Anniversary** | **[CANON]** The **large "S" on the hat is a reference to The Spectre** |
| **Milestone IV** | **[CANON]** **Neon yellow Valkyrie wings** projected on his visor |
| **Pizza Man**, **Monster**, **Milkman**, **Retro** | Cosmetic variants |

The **Anniversary** skin is the one that should make you sit up: a direct, wiki-confirmed **Spectre reference on a cosmetic for a Survivor.** That is not something the other survivors have.

And **wings recur across his skins** — Messenger's winged visor, Milestone IV's Valkyrie wings. For a pizza delivery boy, that is a strange motif to keep returning to.

## 7. The Elliot problem

The fandom's most-discussed survivor theory, and the one that most deserves to be taken seriously — while still being **[THEORY]**, not canon.

### The claims

1. Elliot has, or had, some **relationship with The Spectre**.
2. He may have been a **messenger** figure for it.
3. He **betrayed** it and was punished.
4. **[Weakest]** The Elliot players control isn't the real one; the real Elliot is imprisoned elsewhere.

### The evidence

| Evidence | Strength |
|:--|:--|
| The **Anniversary** skin's "S" is a **wiki-confirmed Spectre reference** | **Strong** — official, and unique among survivors |
| A skin literally named **Messenger**, with wings | **Moderate** — naming is deliberate |
| **Milestone IV's Valkyrie wings** — wings as a recurring motif | **Moderate** |
| **Game Theory's** link to **Arke**, the Greek messenger goddess who **betrayed Zeus, was stripped of her wings, and was cast into the underworld** | **Moderate** — an outside reading, but it fits the wing iconography and the "fell from favour into an underworld" shape almost exactly |
| Community claims about soul-merging with a sadistic messenger and a written-out fate | **Weak** — unsourced fan elaboration |

### The honest assessment

The wing motif is real and repeated. The Spectre reference on his cosmetic is real and official. The Arke parallel is genuinely apt: a **messenger who betrayed the ruler, lost their wings, and was sent below** is an unnervingly precise description of "a character with wings on his skins who ends up in The Spectre's underworld."

But every single piece of it lives in **cosmetics**, and cosmetics are the one category of Forsaken content the wiki explicitly labels non-canonical.

**Verdict:** the strongest *aesthetically* supported theory in the game and the weakest *narratively* supported one. Something is being seeded. What it is has not been said. Full breakdown in [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 4.

## 8. The story, assembled

> He was born into money and never really used it. His mother died, and after that it was him, his father, and his little sister Mia — so Elliot decided, somewhere young, that he would be the one who carried things.
>
> He went to work at the family pizzeria and then did every job in it. Delivery, cashier, kitchen, whatever was short that day. Employee of the month for so long the title stopped meaning anything. He could have lived off his father's success. He wanted to build his own.
>
> And exploiters kept destroying the place. Over and over. 007n7 first, and later a ten-year-old boy called c00lkidd, who one day pointed a rocket launcher at the cashier's desk while Elliot was standing behind it. Both of them got banned. Neither of them had to think about it again.
>
> Elliot cleaned up and went back to work.
>
> Then, one ordinary shift, he took the shop scooter out on a delivery and crashed, and something caught him mid-fall.
>
> The scooter came with him — bent, twisted, useless. He keeps working on it in his free time. He is not going to fix it. He works on it anyway.
>
> He is a healer here, and he is very good at it, because his whole life was practice. His abilities cannot heal him; they only heal other people, and the only way he gets stronger is by helping someone else first.
>
> And every round he shares a map with the man who used to destroy his workplace, and gets hunted by the child who shot at him, and he heals them both when the Realm lets him.
>
> There is a large letter S on one of his hats, and it stands for the thing that owns this place. He has wings on two of his outfits. Nobody has explained either.

## 9. Open questions

1. **What happened to Mia and Mr. Builder?** Two named living relatives, no documented fate.
2. **What is `Elliot/Friend`?** A documented page not covered here — check it live.
3. **Why the "S"?** An official Spectre reference on a Survivor cosmetic.
4. **Why wings, twice?**
5. **Does Elliot know 007n7 was one of the exploiters?** 007n7 clearly knows, and feels it.
6. **Who does Elliot blame?** The lore never gives him anger. That may be the point, or it may be a gap.
