## 1. Who Noob is

Noob is the game's entry point, and the developers used that position deliberately: the first character you play is the one with the least power, the least knowledge, and the most ordinary life destroyed.

> **[CANON]** "Noob is a shy Robloxian who enjoys eating food and hanging out with their friends... a very friendly person who enjoys fashion and hanging out with their friends. They often get startled easily, but in spite of everything that's thrown at them, they still press on, hoping to someday find an escape from their purgatory."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Noob*](https://forsaken.wiki/Noob)

Note the word **purgatory**. It is the wiki's own word, in Noob's own character description, and it is one of the strongest pieces of evidence for the afterlife reading of the Realm.

Noob is not a comic-relief character. Noob is what Forsaken looks like when it happens to someone with no special skills, no dark past, and nothing to atone for except a friendship they couldn't save.

### Real-world basis

Noob is based on the **default Roblox avatar from 2006–2007** — the yellow-headed, blue-and-green "Figure3" body that an entire generation of players started as. The choice matters: Noob is *literally* the player. Not a celebrity admin, not a legendary exploiter, not a machinima protagonist. The baseline. The person the game happens to.

## 2. Life before the Realm — the Sixer story

Noob's backstory is entirely, and only, about one friendship. The whole tragedy hinges on it.

### Meeting Sixer

**Guest 666** was demonised from the start, purely because of the number in their name. Robloxians invented myths and legends about them terrorising the population, and the alienation was compounded by the terror campaign of the **Bacon Empire**. The result was a person who grew up **profoundly lonely**, with nobody willing to be their friend.

> **[CANON]** "Eventually Noob would meet Guest 666, someone who was not frightened by the myths, and the two would become incredibly close, hanging out nearly every day and playing games with one another, with Noob giving them the nickname 'Sixer'."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Guest 666*](https://forsaken.wiki/Guest_666)

The nickname is the heart of the character. The wiki is explicit about its function:

> **[CANON]** Noob gave Guest 666 the nickname **"Sixer"** to show they see her **for who she is**, and to help her get over the burden of Guest 666's past.

That is an act of deliberate kindness. Noob looked at someone the entire world had decided was a demon, decided the number was not the person, and gave them a name that wasn't cursed. For a while it worked.

### The envy

But the friendship didn't fix the underlying problem, because the problem wasn't Noob — it was everyone else, and what everyone else had already done to Sixer.

Guest 666 **kept losing.** Every game they played together, Sixer lost. The wiki is careful here: **this did not bother Noob at all.** It bothered Sixer. The envy others had aimed at her all her life turned inward and became envy of her own best friend.

And crucially, Sixer's motive was **not** malice toward Noob. It was the opposite:

> **[CANON]** "Not wanting to lose their relationship with Noob, Guest 666 would obtain script injectors that they would use on themselves to gain an advantage in games."

She cheated **to keep the friendship.** She believed she had to be good enough to deserve Noob, so she injected scripts **directly into her own body** to stop losing.

### The falling out

Noob found out. Noob called her out on it.

> **[CANON]** "Guest 666, feeling betrayed by Noob, would completely cut contact and abandon them."

That is the entire break. Noob did the correct, decent thing — confronted a friend who was cheating and hurting themself — and it ended the friendship permanently.

**[SECONDARY]** TV Tropes places the confrontation **at a beach**, and characterises the whole sequence as **miscommunication**: Sixer altered her body *to impress Noob*, Noob read it as cheating, and the gap between those two readings destroyed both of them.

### The aftermath

> **[CANON]** "Noob would silently wish for Guest 666 to reconnect with them one day, believing **they were the reason he went missing**, and regretting how they acted towards her that day."

This is Noob's qualifying grief, and it is exactly the flavour The Spectre feeds on: **guilt over an outcome that was not actually your fault.** Noob did nothing wrong. Noob believes otherwise, permanently.

**[THEORY / community]** A widely-repeated claim holds that Noob wrote an **apology letter to Guest 666 that was never sent**. Related: the ARG surfaced **encoded letters apparently written by Guest 666**, expressing pain about being abandoned by Noob. The ARG letters are documented; the unsent-letter detail is community-level and should be treated as unconfirmed.

If both are real, it is the cruellest possible arrangement: two people each privately convinced the other abandoned them, each writing letters the other will never read, now trapped in the same Realm on opposite sides of a knife.

## 3. What Sixer became

Noob's best friend is now one of the monsters hunting them.

> **[CANON]** "Guest 666 is a Guest Robloxian who became a bloodthirsty beast after being Forsaken. Guest 666 would constantly lose whatever games she played, which caused resentment and envy to grow within her. Eventually she would obtain script injectors that she injected directly into herself, giving her abilities."
> — [Official Forsaken Wiki, *Guest 666*](https://forsaken.wiki/Guest_666)

The script injectors she used to save the friendship are **the same power she now kills with**. The Spectre didn't have to invent her weapon. It just took what her loneliness had already made and let it finish the job.

**[THEORY]** The Phosphorus / Seven Deadly Sins theory assigns Guest 666 **Envy**, citing exactly this backstory. See [`13-theories.md`](13-theories.md), Theory 3.

## 4. How Noob was Forsaken

**Unknown.** Noob is one of the many survivors whose abduction is undocumented.

What we do know is that Noob fits the stated criterion — **[CANON]** The Spectre "takes people based on **low moments in life** and/or **high emotions and fears**" — and Noob, carrying unresolved grief and self-blame over a lost friend, is squarely a low moment. Noob's own character description calls the Realm their **"purgatory."**

Worth stressing, since the first draft of this compendium blurred it: **the criterion is not guilt.** Noob did nothing wrong. They told a friend the truth about cheating. The Spectre does not appear to check whether the anguish is deserved.

**[THEORY]** A circulating community claim states Noob was forsaken "during a dramatic incident with Guest 666." Plausible given that the falling-out is the only significant event in Noob's documented life, but **not sourced to the official wiki**.

## 5. In the Realm

Noob is the roster's emotional baseline. Easily startled, kind, sociable, still hungry, still interested in fashion — someone trying to hold onto an ordinary personality inside a death loop.

> **[CANON]** "In spite of everything that's thrown at them, they still press on."

**[SECONDARY]** TV Tropes notes that despite becoming friends with other survivors, Noob **remains isolated and lonely.** They have company. They do not have *their person*.

## 6. The kit as characterisation

Noob is a **Survivalist**: self-preservation, evasion, staying alive rather than fighting.

| Ability | Effect | What it says |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Ghost Burger** | Grants invisibility to slip past Killers | Noob's answer to danger is to *not be seen* |
| **Bloxy Cola** | Speed I for 10s | Run |
| **Slateskin Potion** | Slateskin II for 6s | Endure the hit, keep running |

Every one of Noob's tools is **consumable food and drink** — the classic Roblox gear items, and the exact things the wiki says Noob enjoys in ordinary life. Noob does not fight back. Noob has no weapon. Noob's entire kit is *snacks*, repurposed into survival items.

**[SECONDARY]** TV Tropes notes Noob's play is about "using correct foods in correct situations," with a unique trait of **stacking Bloxy Colas.**

There is a quiet thematic point here: the ordinary person's survival strategy is to take the small comforts of their old life and make them into armour.

## 8. The story, assembled

> A perfectly ordinary Robloxian — shy, friendly, fond of food and clothes — met the most hated person on the platform and decided the hatred was nonsense.
>
> Guest 666 had been a monster in other people's stories since before anyone met her. Noob didn't believe the stories. Noob gave her a nickname that wasn't a number, and for a while she got to be a person instead of a legend.
>
> But she kept losing. Every game. And the envy that the whole world had aimed at her turned inward, and she got it into her head that she had to *win* to be worth keeping. So she got script injectors and put them into her own body, because she was frightened of losing the one friend she had.
>
> Noob found out and said something. That was the right thing to do. It was also the end.
>
> She cut contact completely. Then she disappeared. And Noob — who had done nothing but be honest with a friend — decided it was their fault, and has been carrying that ever since.
>
> Something that eats guilt noticed.
>
> Now Noob is in a place their own file calls a purgatory, running from killers with a burger and a bottle of cola, still shy, still startled, still pressing on. And sometimes the thing hunting them through the dark is wearing their best friend's face, holding the power she bought to keep them.

## 9. Open questions

1. **How was Noob Forsaken?** The single biggest gap.
2. **Do Noob and Guest 666 recognise each other in the Realm?** Unconfirmed. The Two Time/Azure precedent — where The Spectre actively *forbids* forgiveness — suggests recognition without resolution is the likely design.
3. **What is in the ARG letters?** Encoded Guest 666 letters exist; full decoded content is community-held.
4. **Is Noob's genderfluidity on the official wiki?** TV Tropes states it as Forsaken canon; developer **Vess** has confirmed character pronouns on the Discord. The Discord confirmation is the authority here.
5. **Did Noob ever write back?** The unsent letter is the fandom's favourite detail and the least confirmed.
